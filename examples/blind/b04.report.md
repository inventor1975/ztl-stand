  box enclosure is exact for this problem (T appears nowhere else). Effect: safe (outward rounded); but the
  Arrhenius law itself, T, R, Tref, Ea are NOT in the brick, so the stand did not check them.
- Units: h and mol are not in the unit list, so Q was converted to 1/1800 m3/s and mol was treated as a pure
  count (Cin = 2000 1/m3, dH = 70000 J). Dimensional check passes but only because mol is erased. Neutral.
- |dH|: given the magnitude directly (no abs() in laws). Neutral.
- "Q appears in tau and in q" and "Cin in Cout and X": stand treats each occurrence independently in interval
  bounds (no correlation). Safer (conservative), may wrongly refuse a good vessel.
