# b05 — Backup battery for a telecom cabinet (blind test report)

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Load P = 300 W +-10%, terminal V = 11.5..12.8 V, I = P/V (a `define`, division by toleranced givens accepted).
- H = 20 h (as 72000 s), charger Ich = 15 A +-5%, recharge law t_ch = 0.9*1.15*C_rated/Ich, requirement t_ch <= 12 h.
- Two modes: warm (factor 1) and cold (factor as range 0.77..0.83, i.e. 0.80 +-0.03 absolute); runtime >= 2.5 h required in each mode.
- Mass <= 45 kg (via law M == BAT.mass, since requirements need a derived quantity). Catalog B1..B4 with C_rated +-5%, mass, price. Objective: minimize price.

## APPROXIMATED
- Peukert t = H*(C_eff/(I*H))^k has a variable exponent; the format has no power/log. Used x = C_eff/(I*H) (always < 0.38 < 1) and the
  bound x^k >= x^(k+0.02) (x<1, k tolerance folded in at its worst end), then replaced x^(k+0.02) by its TANGENT line a0 + a1*x taken at
  the worst-case x (convex, so the tangent is a lower bound). a0, a1 were computed outside the format (python) for each item and mode
  and rounded DOWN, stored as catalog fields aw0/aw1 (warm) and ac0/ac1 (cold). Two derived runtimes tw, tc, each required only in its own mode.
  Effect: SAFER (sufficient, never optimistic) in the runtime check; tight at the worst corner, looser elsewhere. The k tolerance is no
  longer a toleranced quantity inside the stand (k is a label-like field, unused by any law).
- Hours and Ah are not units: everything in seconds and A*s (100 Ah = 360000 A*s); requirements written as 9000 s and 43200 s.
- "Cold mode factor +-0.03" modelled as an absolute range; as I read the problem that is what it means.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- A power law with a variable (toleranced) exponent: x^k, or log/exp at all. Needed here for Peukert; the user must hand-linearize outside the
  format, and the soundness of that step (convexity, x<1, rounding direction) is NOT checked by the stand; it is my claim.
- The tangent trick needs per-item constants, i.e. precomputation by the user; a catalog cannot hold a formula over its own fields.
- Hours / Ah units. Also no way to tie a mode to a derived quantity (I needed two runtime quantities tw, tc and require each per mode).

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
Cold lower end for B3: x = 0.77*0.95*540000 / ((330/11.5)*72000) = 395010/2066087 = 0.191187; tc = 72000*(-0.0292 + 0.84759*0.191187) = 9565.1 s
(matches the tool). The exact power law at that corner (k+0.02 = 1.22) gives 20*0.191187^1.22 = 2.657 h = 9565 s, so the tangent is tight there.
Independent python of all 4 items at worst corners: B1 cold 1.80 h, warm 2.44 h
(fail), B2 cold 2.10 h (fail), B4 cold 2.22 h (fail), B3 cold 2.66 h (pass) — consistent with the tool refuting B1, B2 and choosing B3.
