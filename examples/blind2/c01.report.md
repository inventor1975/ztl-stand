# c01 report: Oil cooler, counter-flow plate exchanger (problem 1)

Final verdict on examples/blind2/c01.json (faithful version): **OPEN** (HX-18, HX-26, HX-40T REFUTED; HX-40 OPEN; no design EARNED).
Own check: HX-40 does pass everywhere (see below), so OPEN here is the stand failing to prove, not a design failure.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- All given ranges: oil flow 2.0 kg/s +-3 %, oil inlet 93..97 C, water flow 1.6 kg/s +-5 %, water inlet 15..28 C, Rf 0.0001..0.0006 m2K/W, cp's.
- Effectiveness-NTU with the exact formula eps = (1-exp(-NTU(1-Cr)))/(1-Cr*exp(-NTU(1-Cr))).
- U scaling: Uclean*(Tmean/80)^0.8*(m/2)^0.5, then 1/U = 1/Uc + Rf. Tmean = (Thi+Tho)/2 depends on the unknown Tho, so Tho is an
  implicit-law root (`search` [15,97]), the way the pump brick does it.
- Water outlet from the heat balance, pressure drop dp0*(m/2)^1.7, the four-model catalog with area/U/dp0/price, three requirements, objective min price, `report: all`.
- Units checked ("every law's dimensions agree"), including kJ/(kg*K), W/(m^2*K), bar, USD.

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Cmin = Ch (oil), Cmax = Cc (water)** instead of min()/max(), and Cr<1 declared as `valid: "Cc - Ch > 0"`. Reason: with min()/max() in the defines the
   checker failed ("definitions could not be bounded"). Safe: the valid condition is a requirement the stand itself checks over the whole box
   (Ch<=4.326, Cc>=6.354 kW/K, so it holds). Checked by the stand. But the Cr=1 branch eps = NTU/(1+NTU) cannot be written (no branching); never reached in these ranges
   (my calc: Cr in [0.580, 0.681]). The problem's remark "Cr can approach 1" is false for these numbers.
2. `search` for Tho = [15, 97] (the physical span water-inlet..oil-inlet). Stand checks there is a root in it; a root outside is not looked for. Safe by physics (my judgement, not checked).
3. Decimal exponents 0.8, 0.5, 1.7 written as decimals in `pow` — worked in the end; fractions did nothing different. Not an approximation, noted only because the first error message pointed at them wrongly (see DOC GAPS).
4. "+-2 K" written as the range [93,97] (same thing). Reference constants 2.0 kg/s and 80 C are given quantities (needed for units).
Nothing else was simplified: no hand algebra, no outside bounds, no dropped couplings.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- min()/max() of two toleranced capacity rates and the Cr = 1 limit branch (piecewise formula). min/max exist as functions but the checker could not bound them here (runs with them: HX-26 and HX-40 OPEN "definitions could not be bounded").
- Nothing else of the stated problem was inexpressible. LMTD form not tried (the ln(dT1/dT2) with dT1=dT2 case is again a branch).

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT

- Nominal Tho HX-18 = 64.67476 C -> overshoot 2.67476: **matches the stand's witness exactly** (all digits shown, ~2.67476404965).
- HX-26: worst corner Tho 66.20 C (> 62, overshoot 4.2), nominal 58.29. Stand's witness 63.354 (overshoot 1.354) is a different, milder point - REFUTED is right; its "by" is the witness, not the worst case (docs say so).
- HX-40T: dp = 1.30 nominal > 1.0, REFUTED right. (Worst 1.367.)
- HX-40: corner max Tho 58.92 C (<=62), Tco max 58.72 C (<=70), dp max 0.99896 bar (<=1.0; margin 0.1 %: 0.95*1.03^1.7). Random interior max 58.09. So HX-40 passes everywhere: true answer = **HX-40, $3 700, cheapest passing**. The stand says OPEN, i.e. consistent but not decisive.
- HX-18 worst corner Tho 72.04. Interesting: stand's OPEN upper bound for HX-40 (72.75) is about HX-18's true worst case: the enclosure is loose by ~14 K.
- Not checked: the stand's internal certificates/ranges beyond these; HX-26's exact witness point; Tco ranges for the other models.

## DOC GAPS
- A define whose name equals a catalog field name (`Uc` vs `HX.Uc`) breaks silently: the define is substituted inside `slot.field`. BRICKS.md should forbid it, and the error should name the cause ("is not a readable expression" on an unrelated law is misleading).
- min/max are listed as functions, but nothing says they can make the checker fail ("definitions could not be bounded") when applied to toleranced products in a define.
- The doc says an implicit law may use the root inside functions/defines but does not say how wide the enclosure gets with ~5 toleranced inputs; here the interior extreme is left undecided and no way is offered to help it (no way to ask for box splitting).
- The "valid" example shows a derived-quantity condition; it is not said that it can also be used for a pure-given condition like `Cc - Ch > 0` (it worked, requirement counts as checked).
- "Nine toleranced quantities take about 20 s per design" - I had five and it took 10-25 s per design for this brick, and 265 s total for a narrowed variant on a loaded machine; times vary a lot.
