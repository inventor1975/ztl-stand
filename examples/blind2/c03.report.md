# c03 report: 18 GHz microwave link, link budget (problem 3)

Verdict on the brick as stated: **REFUTED** (all 48 designs fail the EIRP limit; the stand's answer is right, the problem
as written is infeasible). Run time about 2.8 s.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Distance 4.6-5.4 km (range), L_misc 1.0-2.0 dB, both cable losses 0.7-1.1 dB, rain gamma 0.4-2.6 dB/km, f = 18 GHz.
- FSPL = 92.45 + 20 log10(f/1 GHz) + 20 log10(d/1 km) with the real log10, as a `define` (f/F1 and d/D1 with F1, D1 given
  constants with units, because log10 needs a dimensionless argument).
- Rain loss = gamma * d/(1 + d/35 km), as a `define`.
- Link budget as one law, Margin = Prx - Sens, EIRP = P_tx + G_tx - L_tx as laws.
- Slots: licence (3 items), TX dish and RX dish (same 4-item catalog in two slots, so mixed sizes allowed), 48 designs.
- Modulation switch as two modes: "clear sky 256QAM" (gamma = 0, Sens -66 +-1.5, margin >= 25) and "worst rain 16QAM"
  (gamma 0.4-2.6, Sens -78 +-1.5, margin >= 3). EIRP <= 40 is top-level (applies in both modes).
- Objective: minimize total price, `report: all`.

## APPROXIMATED
- **dBm written as dB.** There is no dBm unit; P_tx, Prx, Sens, EIRP are all "dB". Safe numerically; but nobody checks
  the dBm/dB distinction (a dB-plus-dBm addition would pass; only I checked it). Test: dB + W is a BRICK ERROR, but dB is
  dimensionless, so dB mixed with plain ratios passes silently (and `dB/km` swapped to `1/km` ran without a complaint).
- **Receiver spread +-1.5 dB** is folded into Sens as a range (-67.5..-64.5 and -79.5..-76.5). Correct for the margins.
  For requirement (d) I added a given Uspr = 1.5 dB and a derived PrxHi = Prx + Uspr, required <= -25. My reading of
  "including the +1.5 dB unit spread" (one-sided, on the received level, not on the sensitivity); the text is ambiguous.
  Not checked by anyone.
- **Clear sky = gamma overridden to exactly 0** in that mode. Requirement (d) lives in the 256QAM clear-sky mode
  (it does not depend on modulation; the mode is only a container).
- "Worst rain case" = gamma at the top of its range at the same time as d at the top; the box contains it, the stand
  takes every combination. The problem's "rain rate for the required availability" is simply the range 0.4-2.6.
- Cable-loss tolerance and L_misc taken as independent ranges (as stated); EIRP uses the tx cable only.
- None of these changes is non-conservative as far as I can see, apart from the reading of (d) above.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- The problem's *physical* link between gamma and availability/rain rate (just a range here).
- "The radio chooses the modulation" as a choice by the radio: expressed only as a fixed pairing mode -> sensitivity.
  There is no way to say "the radio picks the best modulation per condition"; I wrote the two cases by hand.
- dBm vs dB vs dBi as distinct units; logarithmic arithmetic (dB are just numbers here).
- A "bound is a derived quantity" is not allowed, so the (d) bound had to be restated through PrxHi; fine, not a loss.
- Nothing essential was missing otherwise. Spread of the antenna gain (none was stated) not modelled.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
- Checked by python: min EIRP over the catalog = 18 + 33.5 - 1.1 = 50.4 dBm > 40 for every design. So REFUTED is correct,
  and the stand's witness (18 + 33.5 - 0.9 = 50.6, over by 10.6) matches exactly. The problem statement is infeasible as
  written (probably the 40 dBm is a data error: any 18 GHz dish >= 33.5 dBi at 18 dBm already gives ~51 dBm).
- Variant: my own exhaustive scan (worst-corner arithmetic, FSPL at 5.4 km = 132.2033 dB, at 4.6 km = 130.8106) gives exactly the same 6 passing
  designs and the same cheapest one (1560). Clear-sky minimum margin 25.0967 matches my 25.0967.
- Rain-mode minimum margin: my corner calculation gives 24.9334; the stand says 24.6876. The difference 0.2458 is from
  Lrain = gam*d/(1+d/Dc) being bounded by naive interval arithmetic (d appears twice: 2.6*5.4/(1+4.6/35) = 12.41 vs the true 12.16).
  So the stand's rain-mode range is sound but pessimistic by 0.25 dB, and the "checker's own bound" repeats the same wide value, so the
  checker is not an independent corner check there. Harmless here (margin needs only >= 3), but a tighter requirement could come out OPEN.
- Not checked: the PrxHi bound by hand (its reported -34.71 equals my -34.71 for the nominal-corner arithmetic; I did not recheck other designs).

## DOC GAPS
- Nothing in BRICKS.md says whether `dB`/`dBm` are valid units or what scale they have; they work as plain dimensionless numbers,
  and `dB` mixes silently with dimensionless and with the swapped `1/km`. A sentence would help.
- Not stated that a design failing at its nominal point is dropped before any other requirement or mode is looked at, so a
  REFUTED answer can hide that the remaining requirements are fine (this explains the missing `minimal_conflicts`).
- Not stated that a `define` with a repeated toleranced quantity (d twice in `d/(1+d/Dc)`) is bounded by naive intervals and loses the dependency.
- Modes: the doc does not say whether an override with `"value": "0"` for a `range` quantity replaces the range (it did).
- The section says `report: "all"` lists designs but the REFUTED output with 48 designs is long (every design echoes the full text); fine but noisy.
