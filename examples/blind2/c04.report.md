# c04 report: LED indicator on a vehicle supply (problem 4)

Final brick: `examples/blind2/c04.json` (literal reading of the stated test conditions, search `[1,200]` mA).
Verdict: **EARNED** - LED-A + metal film 1 % E96 0.5 W, R = 604 ohm, total $0.17. `optimal: false` ("6 cheaper design(s) undecided").

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Diode law with the "-1": `Vs - n*VT*ln(I/IS + 1) == I*Rt`, as an implicit law on `I` (search 1..200 mA). `VT = kq*Tjk` with `kq = 25.85 mV/300 K` exactly; `IS = Is300*(T/300)^3*exp((Egn/kq)*(1/300 - 1/T))` with Egn = 3.0 V (E_g/n = 3.0 eV, literal). Junction T given as a range 253.15..368.15 K.
- Resistor: value from a catalog, relative tolerance (5 % / 1 %), tempco as a `range` (+-200 / +-50 ppm/K), `Rt = R*(1 + tc*dTr)`, dTr = resistor temperature minus 25 C (ambient -40..85 C -> -65..+60).
- Four modes (stand EARNED only if all hold, for every value of the box):
  - crank: Vs = 10.8, Tj = -20 C, ambient -40 C, `I >= 8 mA`;
  - nominal: Vs = 13.6, Tj = 25 C, resistor at 25 C, `I within [14, 20] mA`;
  - alternator: Vs = 14.8, cold junction (as written), `I <= 0.9*Imax`;
  - transient: Vs = 16, `I <= Imax` and `P_R <= 0.7*P_rated`, over the whole Tj and dTr range.
- Catalog: LED-A/B/C (n, Is, Imax, price, name label) exactly as given. Resistors: E24 5 % 0.25 W $0.01; E96 1 % 0.25 W $0.03; E96 1 % 0.5 W $0.05. Objective: LED.price + R.price. 3 x (24 + 96 + 96) = 648 designs; 196 were judged before the first EARNED one.

## APPROXIMATED
1. **E96 had to be written as 96 listed items** (the stand knows only E12/E24: `BRICK ERROR: unknown series 'E96'`). I typed the E96 row from memory (generated by script; `assert len == 96`, not checked against a standard). Only the stand checks the arithmetic; the E96 table values are on me. Safe if my table is right.
2. **Decade restricted to 100..976 ohm (E24: 100..910)** by my own hand argument (I need ~17 mA from ~13.6-3.3 V -> ~600 ohm). Not checked by the stand. Not conservative as a proof of optimality: values outside this decade are simply not in the catalog (R < 100 ohm would exceed Imax; R >= 1 k would fail 14 mA, by my reasoning only).
3. **"Cold junction" for 14.8 V taken literally (Tj = -20 C).** Physically cold gives the LOWEST current (Vf rises); the worst case for over-current is HOT. I also ran a variant with the full Tj/dTr range in the 14.8 V mode (see TOOL OUTPUT) - same winner. Literal version is NOT conservative; the full version is.
4. **Temperatures coupled only in modes 1-3** (Tj = -20 with ambient -40). In the 16 V mode Tj and the resistor temperature are independent boxes (conservative: includes combinations that cannot occur together). Resistor self-heating and LED self-heating beyond the stated Tj range: ignored.
5. **Derating: 0.7 x rating applied to ALL resistors, including the 0.5 W part, and at ALL temperatures** in the 16 V mode. The text says only "0.25 W parts ... at 85 C ambient". Conservative for 0.5 W (the problem is ambiguous). Only I decided this.
6. **Search window for I = [1, 200] mA.** With [1, 100] the R = 100/102 ohm designs (I > 100 mA) came out OPEN ("no root in the search range"), not REFUTED, which also made `optimal` false. Widening fixed that. A hand-chosen window; the stand does tell you when a design has no root in it.
7. LED forward voltage from the formulas, not from the quoted "Vf ~ 3.0 V / 3.1 V" (see DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT).
8. 5 % E24 nominal case: tempco has no effect at 25 C (dTr = 0 exact) - consistent with the reference temperature, not an approximation.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
- I wrote an independent float model (bisection on the same implicit equation, exp/ln in python floats, corners of R tol x tc x Tj x dTr) and judged ALL 196 designs the stand judged, for the literal and the full variant. **No disagreement** with any REFUTED/EARNED verdict; the 6 OPEN ones are truly feasible, the 5 R = 100/102 ones (search [1,100] run) are truly infeasible.
- Winner R = 604, my corners: crank min 11.5718 mA, nominal [16.8739, 17.2130], alternator (cold) max 18.5592, full-range 14.8 V max 20.39 mA, 16 V max I 22.40 mA, max power 0.2989 W. Stand's tight ranges (crank, nominal, alternator) match mine to 6 digits. The stand's transient numbers (I up to 23.78 mA, Pr up to 0.346 W) are looser, sound outer bounds above my corner maxima (22.40 mA, 0.299 W). Bound is 0.35 W: passes either way.
- Why the cheaper groups fail: the 0.25 W parts (derated to 0.175 W) dissipate ~0.29 W at 16 V hot -> refuted; 5 % carbon fails mainly the 14.8 V / nominal windows. Plausible.
- **Data inconsistency in the problem, not in the tool**: with the stated formulas Vf at 20 mA, 300 K is 3.30 V (A), 3.54 V (B), 3.43 V (C), not "about 3.0 / 3.1 V". The stated "about -2 mV/K" does not follow from `E_g/n = 3.0 eV`: the formula gives -9.5 mV/K (A), -11.8 (B), -8.1 (C). I used the formulas. The strong tempco makes cold/hot current swings larger than the author intended.
- Not checked: the E96 table against a standard; the reading of "E_g/n effective 3.0 eV" (the author may have meant something else); the choice to derate the 0.5 W part.
