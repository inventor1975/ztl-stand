# c07 report: Maize irrigation over one season (problem 7)

Verdict on the brick as written (`examples/blind2/c07.json`): **REFUTED, all 5 method/pump combinations**, on the
water licence alone. Closest: drip (P1 or P2), worst gross volume 78 575.7 m3 against 78 000 m3 (0.74 % over). A
relaxed twin (licence 1.0 m3/m2, kept only in the scratchpad, not a deliverable) gave EARNED: sprinkler+P2, worst-case
10-year cost USD 47 643.9, `optimal: true`.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Four monthly ET0 values, each +-10 % (independent givens, units mm/day).
- Rainfall 60-180 mm (range); effective fraction 0.70-0.80 (range); Pe = Rain*feff as a `define`.
- Water balance Hnet = max(ETc - Pe - Store, 0) as a law with `max`; gross volume Vg = Hnet*A/eff.
- Efficiencies as catalog `range` fields: drip 0.87-0.93, sprinkler 0.70-0.80, furrow 0.47-0.63.
- Licence 6500 m3/ha x 12 ha as the bound `Lic*A` (unit-checked expression).
- Peak day: Qreq = Kc(1.15)*ET0_jul*A/(eff*areafrac*18 h/day), required <= pump rated flow. ET0_jul is the SAME
  given as in the seasonal sum, so the July tolerance is shared exactly. Unit `h/day` made the 18 h/day check real.
- Energy: Etot = kW*Vg/Q (pump at rated flow and listed kW), bounded by 2800 kWh/ha * A.
- Catalog of 5 listed items (eff, Q, kW, capex, areafrac), one slot. Objective: worst-case 10-year cost
  `Cost10 == capex + 10*0.14*Etot`, `"case":"worst"`, `"report":"all"`. Units checked ("every law's dimensions agree").

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Kc curve summed by hand (NOT safe to trust blindly; checked only by me).** The format has no sums or piecewise
   functions, so I computed per-month sums of daily Kc in Python with exact fractions and entered them as givens
   `S_may..S_aug` (11.33, 25.22, 35.65, 26.425 days). The day convention is my choice: Kc=0.35 for days 1-25,
   ramp 0.35 + 0.8*(d-25)/35 for days 26-60 (1.15 at day 60), 1.15 for 61-100, ramp down to 0.60 at day 120;
   months are days 1-31, 32-61, 62-92, 93-120. A different ramp-end convention shifts ETc by a few mm (a fraction of
   a percent, comparable to the 0.74 % margin that decides the verdict). The stand cannot check this step.
2. **Seasonal balance instead of daily.** Net depth = max(0, season ETc - effective rain - store). No daily soil
   moisture, no timing of rain, no runoff. Rain timing could only make things worse, so this is optimistic, not
   conservative. Checked by nobody.
3. **Soil store 40 mm fully credited as an exact value.** Statement says "may be used (no more than 40)"; that is
   ambiguous. Crediting all 40 is the optimistic reading; a range 0-40 would worsen every design. I did not run it.
4. **"90 % (+-3)" read as +-3 percentage points** (0.87-0.93), likewise +-5 and +-8 points, not relative +-3 %.
   Relative would give 0.873-0.927: slightly narrower. Judge's choice, not the stand's.
5. **Furrow 80 % area limit** modeled as dividing the peak flow requirement by 0.8 (`areafrac`). Other reading:
   100 % delivery on 100 % area impossible, so furrow is refuted outright. Furrow is refuted anyway by the licence.
6. **Soil store not used in the peak day** ("no rain" taken to include no store): conservative.
7. **Energy and cost over 10 seasons assume the worst-case season repeats 10 times** (worst-case objective). A cost
   "at nominal weather" is not expressible (nominal objective cannot read derived Etot); the worst-case cost can rank
   differently from the expected cost, though here capex dominates.
8. ha is not a unit: area in m2, licence 0.65 m, energy limit 0.28 kWh/m2 (converted by hand; trivially checkable).
9. Electric energy per ha uses delivered volume / rated flow; no pump curve, no efficiency drop off-duty point
   (as stated in the problem).

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- Piecewise-linear Kc and day-by-day / month-by-month summation (done outside, item 1 above). No sum/for-each, no
  `if`/piecewise function.
- A daily time structure at all (soil moisture store dynamics, rain timing, "peak 10 days" as a window inside the
  season rather than a separate steady-state check).
- "May be used, no more than 40 mm" as a design choice rather than a fixed value or an all-values range.
- Nominal (expected) 10-year cost as the objective: only the worst-case-over-box cost is available.
- Per-ha reporting (`ha` unit absent), so numbers come back in m3 / kWh totals.
- The "for every combination" licence requirement is a single all-corners worst, so the result 0.74 % over is driven
  by every month being +10 % at once together with the lowest rain; the format cannot express a correlated or
  probabilistic ET0 (all months high together is arguably unlikely) - not a format fault, but worth saying.

## DOC GAPS

  violation but is only a witness value (hit while comparing the 977 vs 19 658 overshoots).
- `how` prints the checker's own bound for Etot in SI base units (joules, 4.79e10) while the quantity is in kWh
  and the other ranges are in the quantity's unit; looks like a mismatch for a reader.
- BRICKS.md does not say a toleranced catalog field can use `{"range": ...}` inside `items` with a sibling plain
  string field (it is there, but only by the one-line "a field is either ..."); worked fine.
- `h/day` and `kWh/m^2` as compound units work but the unit list does not say `kWh` is accepted by the prefix rule
  (k + Wh) - it was, and `ha` is not.
- A bound expression's names are in SI is stated, but results appear in SI inside witness points (ET0 as ~1e-8 m/s
  though declared mm/day), which is confusing when reading `its_violating_point`.
- Nothing said about whether `max(x, 0)` accepts a bare 0 next to a dimensioned argument; it worked.
