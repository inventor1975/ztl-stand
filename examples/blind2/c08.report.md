# c08 report: Spare-part reorder policy (problem 8)

Brick: `examples/blind2/c08.json` (generated by a small python script; 819 catalog items = 3 suppliers x all pack-multiple
order quantities up to 2100 x 3 service levels, one slot `POL`). Final verdict: EARNED, optimal true, S2, Q=1000, SS=446
(98 %), proved worst-case annual cost 137 712.3 USD.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Demand D in [10800, 13200], order cost S = 45 +-5 (tolerance 1/9), holding rate in [0.19, 0.25]; all as givens.
- Price breaks and pack sizes: each (supplier, Q) is one catalog item carrying the price of its regime, L, sigma_L. Pack
  multiples and break regimes are therefore exact by construction; the EOQ may lie outside its bracket (nothing forces it in).
- Service-level requirement over the whole demand range: `zeff == SS/sigLT(D)`, `sigLT = sqrt(L*sd^2 + (D/250)^2*sL^2)`,
  required `>= 2.054` (the table value for 98 %). Function `sqrt` used directly in the brick.
- Capacity `peak == Q + SS <= 2100`; cash `cash == (Q/2 + SS)*p <= 15000`.
- Total cost law `TC == D*p + (D/Q)*S + (Q/2+SS)*hr*p` and the objective "minimize TC, case worst" (worst over D, S, hr).
- EOQ `sqrt(2DS/H)` as an informational derived quantity: range [582.09, 825.30] for p=10.2 (stand's judge_log).

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Safety stock rounded up to an integer, computed OUTSIDE the stand** (python, exact: smallest integer n with
   n^2 >= z^2 * sigma_LT^2 at D = 13200, for each supplier and z = 1.645 / 2.054 / 2.326), entered as catalog field `SS`.
   Conservative (rounding up) and it is what a warehouse does anyway. Who checks: the stand checks the RESULT
   (zeff >= 2.054 over the demand range, peak, cash), so a wrong SS cannot be EARNED; it could only be too large and
   miss a cheaper design (I recomputed it by hand: matches 129.3->130, 445.09->446, 73.63->74).
   Why not the literal `SS = z*sqrt(...)` formula: I first wrote that (z a catalog slot). Result: every 98 % design came
   out OPEN (257 designs), zeff proved within [2.054, 2.34456], because at D = 13200 the requirement holds with EXACT
   equality and the sqrt bracket is ~1e-14 wide, so the lower end sits a hair under the bound; the best policy was then
   wrongly the 99 % one (S2 Q=1000, 138 k). Not a stand fault, but the literal statement is not decidable by the stand.
2. **Order quantity candidates enumerated by hand into the catalog** (all pack multiples 50/100/10 up to 2100, regimes
   split at the breaks). Upper limit 2100 is safe (Q+SS<=2100 would refute any larger Q anyway). Q is not a free
   continuous choice; the stand only picks among the listed ones.
3. **Dimensions: all counts and days written with unit ""** (no `year`, and units/day vs units/day^0.5 inside
   sigma_LT would not close). Money is in USD and checked; the rest is NOT dimension-checked in a meaningful way.
   Unsafe in the sense that nobody checks e.g. days vs units; I checked by hand.
4. **Demand "at its high end for lead-time demand"** is implemented by computing SS from DH = 13200 (the same value
   baked into the integer SS) while the service requirement uses the actual D; semantically equals the statement.
5. **The question "for each supplier" is not answered by one run.** I ran the full brick (all suppliers) and two scratch
   variants with only S1 / only S3 items (not delivered files) to get per-supplier winners: S1 Q=1500 SS=130 worst 146 718;
   S3 Q=650 SS=74 worst 165 932; S2 Q=1000 SS=446 worst 137 712.3. The stand itself lists at most 200 designs, so it did not show these.
6. Objective is the WORST case (D=13200, S=50, hr=0.25 all increase TC). The nominal-cost optimum can differ (S3: nominal
   best Q=630, worst-case best Q=650; I checked by python only).

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- Choice of the safety stock / order quantity as a free integer variable with a "round up to pack" rule; there is no
  ceiling function, no piecewise price by quantity, no "Q is a multiple of pack". All done by enumeration.
- A statistical service level: Phi(z) is not available; the normal table z is entered as given numbers.
- "Per supplier" best policy as one report (no group-by / per-slot-value optimum; max 200 designs listed).
- Years, "units per day" as a proper dimension.
- A requirement that holds with equality at a corner is OPEN, not EARNED, when a function is involved (see 1).
