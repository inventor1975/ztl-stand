# Blind test 3 of the input format — results (2026-10-10)

**Setup.** Same protocol as rounds 1 and 2, on twelve domains that appear in neither, after the round-2 gaps (H1-H14)
were closed or narrowed and ZTL learned the affine reading and parameter partitions:

- A separate agent that never saw the format wrote 12 problems (`PROBLEMS.md`), asked to include regimes, tables,
  sums over tables, hidden common variables, operating points, worst cases and integer choices.
- Twelve blind authors (Sonnet) each got BRICKS.md, the example bricks and one problem; no access to the code, the
  tests, GAPS.md or rounds 1-2; each with its own scratch folder (in round 2 two authors overwrote each other).
- Each ran the stand under 3 GB and 15 minutes, wrote `cNN.json` and `cNN.report.md`.
- All 12 bricks were re-run by Logik1 (see the end). The c11 author left a scratch variant `c11_notol_tmp.json`
  in this folder against the instructions; it was removed (the delivered `c11.json` is untouched).

often to the printed digits. Six problems were REFUTED by their own invented data (the authors confirmed each).

Compared with rounds 1 and 2 — the brick held everywhere and the expression language was enough for the physics;
the implicit operating point with a piecewise motor curve (c5) and the branch (c11 Sabine/Eyring) went in as stated.
What remains is again the SEARCH (continuous and large integer spaces) and a few vocabulary items.
