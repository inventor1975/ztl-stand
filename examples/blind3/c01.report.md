# c01 report: Spring/rubber mounts for a screw compressor skid (problem 1)

Brick: `examples/blind3/c01.json` (12 designs = 6 mount types x N in {4,6}, `"report": "all"`).
Verdict on the problem as stated: **REFUTED** (no design satisfies all requirements). Minimal conflict reported: {(a) T<=0.06, (d) Tpeak<=12}.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Mass m = 1200 kg +-5% (`value`+`tolerance`); speed n = 1350..1500 rpm as a `range` in unit `rpm`; f = n via `define` (rpm is read as 1/60 Hz, so no /60).
- Catalog row = one listed item with fields k (value + tolerance 0.15, N/mm), zeta (`range`), Fmin, Fmax (N), price (EUR), label `type`. k and zeta of one mount come from the SAME row, as the statement demands (the row is the pairing).
- N in {4,6} as a second slot with two listed items (not `integers`, which would give 5).
- ktot = N*k as `define`; fn = sqrt(ktot/m)/(2 pi); r = f/fn; T full formula (1+(2 zeta r)^2)/((1-r^2)^2+(2 zeta r)^2) under sqrt, as written; Tpeak = sqrt(1+4 zeta^2)/(2 zeta) (worst at smallest zeta, since the requirement is over the whole zeta range); Fst = m g/N; dst = m g/ktot; g = 9.81 as a given (not g0).
- Requirements (a) T<=0.06 for all m,k,zeta,n; (b) Fst within [Fmin,Fmax] as two requirements with slot.field bounds; (c) dst<=25 mm; (d) Tpeak<=12 and fn<=0.4*f (bound expression; its least favourable end of f is 1350 rpm = f_min); objective = N*price (nominal).

## APPROXIMATED
1. `fref` = 1 Hz given constant inside the fn law: `fn == fref*sqrt(ktot/(m*fref*fref))/(2*pi)`. Needed only to give sqrt a dimensionless argument (I assumed sqrt requires it; I did NOT test whether a dimensional argument works). Mathematically exact, not conservative-or-not; units checked by stand.
2. (c) "static deflection <= 25 mm for the heaviest case": I took ALL allowed values (m max AND k at -15%), the stricter reading. Neither reading changes any verdict (S1 N=4 fails at nominal-k 25.8 mm too). Checked by the stand as written, but the choice of reading is mine.
3. Tpeak as a derived quantity from the full zeta interval: the "smallest zeta" is not coded as such; it is the stand's range over zeta (equivalent, since Tpeak decreases in zeta).
4. Correlations T uses r (from m,k,n) and zeta: the stand drops dependency between occurrences (interval reading of Tr and fn labelled "outside ZTL"). In the one EARNED variant the bound was [0.0202, 0.0410] vs. my grid max 0.0395: sound, a bit wide. Safe (conservative) for EARNING; could in principle give a false OPEN/REFUTED only via witness, which the checker recomputes exactly.
5. Nothing else rounded; numbers are exact strings.

## DOC GAPS
- Not stated whether `rpm` converts to SI as 1/60 Hz (it does: f == n used directly gave right results). A bare `/60` would have been a silent error.
- Not stated whether `sqrt` accepts a dimensional argument (I avoided it with `fref`; sqrt is listed among functions needing a dimensionless argument only by implication from "transcendental").
- Not stated that a `where`-free slot with two listed items serves for "N in {4,6}" (obvious, but `integers` is the only documented N example).
- `Tr` and `fn` are reported "outside ZTL" even though they are only sqrt of rational expressions; the doc suggests functions are bracketed by ZTL, so the label is a surprise.
- It is not documented that a `report: all` brick with REFUTED verdict does not print the objective block.
