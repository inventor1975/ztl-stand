# c04 report: Turbine choice for a hill site (problem 4)

Brick: `examples/blind3/c04.json` (generated by a small python script, 25 bin laws + energy law). Verdict: **REFUTED**, all 4 turbines. This is correct for the data as stated: even at the nominal point no turbine reaches 5.5 GWh (best: T4, 4861 MWh).

Design: modes + computed + if() branches.
- Mode "worst case": S, AV, LS, T overridden to ranges; requirement `E >= 5500` MWh.
- Mode "nominal point": base values (all exact); requirement `CF >= 0.22`.
- Objective is a `computed` cost per MWh.
- Per-bin power is a law `Pi == if(...)` of nested branches; `v_eq^3 = (s*v_c)^3 * rho/rho0`, so no cube root is needed.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Catalog T1..T4 (Pr, vin, vr, vout, price).
- Piecewise power curve (0 below vin, cubic ramp, flat Pr, 0 from vout on) as nested `if`, evaluated on 25 bin centres with the hours given.
- ISA pressure with `pow(1 - AL*HH, 5.25588)` and `rho = p/(287.05*T)`, with T = 278.15 K, range 275.15 ... 281.15 K.
- Density correction.
- Wind factor 0.93 ... 1.07, availability 0.94 ... 0.98, losses 6 ... 10%, all as boxes.
- "Net energy >= 5.5 GWh for every combination" (worst-case mode, over the full box).
- "Capacity factor >= 0.22 at the nominal point" (nominal mode).
- Units checked everywhere.

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Cube root removed algebraically.** `v_eq^3 = s^3 v^3 rho/rho0` is exact, and the comparisons against vin^3, vr^3 and vout^3 are equivalent for positive speeds. Safe; I did it by hand, and the stand did not check it.
2. **Meaning of "hours per bin shift accordingly".** I took it as: the hours stay with their bin and the speed is multiplied by s (P is evaluated at s*v_c). That is not the only reading (one could also re-distribute the hours). It is my choice and unchecked. It affects the numbers.
3. **Nominal objective: `RHO_NOM` is hand-computed.** `computed` has no `pow` or non-integer exponent, so the nominal density 1.0551359049052067 kg/m^3 went in as a plain given quantity. The stand does not tie it to the `RHO` formula; a typo there would silently change the objective ranking. I checked it by python only. The ranking is not conservative or non-conservative, just unchecked.
4. **Duplicate nominal givens `Sn`, `AVn`, `LSn`.** The base values of S, AV and LS are ALSO overridden by the worst-case mode, and `computed` refuses (BRICK ERROR "no exact value") any quantity a mode overrides. So the nominal values exist twice and could drift apart. Unchecked.
5. **Cut-out step in `computed` via `ceil(max(0, min(1, (vout3 - w3)/vout3)))`.** A step function with no `if` available; the nominal energy is therefore a second hand-written copy (25 clamp expressions) of the power curve. Equal to the law version at bin centres (checked by my python: CF values and ordering match). Only I check it; the stand does not compare the two copies.
6. **Objective is evaluated through `computed`, not through the derived `E`.** Ranking only; it never makes a design EARNED. Safe as long as the copy in item 5 is right.
7. **Cost per MWh uses the price in EUR and 20 years x 2.5% O&M.** Exact as stated; I read "20-year total cost" as price + 20*0.025*price.
8. Bare `H1`, `VU` given quantities (1 h, 1 m/s) were needed to give bare numbers a unit: hours-per-bin as a number would be seconds, and in `computed` a bare number is dimensionless. This is only a notation matter.
9. Bin centres 0.5 ... 24.5, hours exactly as listed (they sum to 8760, checked).

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- A **table with a weight per row** (a histogram) as data: 25 laws written out by a script. No sum or array construct.
- A **nominal-point value of a derived quantity in the objective** without rewriting the model a second time in `computed`; the objective cannot name `E` in a mode. The workaround is a full second copy.
- `pow`, `if` or any function in `computed` (no non-integer exponent, no branch), so `rho` at nominal had to be passed in by hand.
- The statement "worst case over the box of a cost per MWh at the nominal energy" is fine, but "cost per MWh" as a ratio of a nominal and a toleranced quantity (e.g. worst-case cost per MWh) is not what `case: worst` does with modes ("not yet with modes").
- Joint question "which turbines satisfy both requirements" - OK as modes, but the output reports only the FIRST failing mode per design (T1/T2 are listed as failing the worst-case mode, T3/T4 the nominal mode; the nominal CF of T1 and T2, which pass, is not shown for refuted designs).
- Hours that depend on s (a different distribution shape) cannot be expressed without per-bin laws in s; here the bin law makes it implicit.

## DOC GAPS
- `computed`: a mode-overridden given counts as "not exact"; BRICKS.md does not say so, and the error message does not name the real cause.
- `computed`: bare numbers are dimensionless (not SI units as in laws) so `(0.125*W)` cannot be compared with a velocity cubed; BRICKS.md says a bare number beside a quantity is an error in `where` but does not say this for sums in `computed`. Also: `pow`/`if` are not listed as unavailable, only implied by the operator list.
- In laws, `if(x, 0, ...)` with a bare 0 against a quantity branch works (I tested). BRICKS.md only says "both branches need the same unit"; a bare `0` seems to be allowed.
- `objective.value` for a computed is returned in SI base units, not the declared unit (doc says results come back in own unit).
- BRICKS.md does not say how a requirement that mixes a worst-case box with a nominal point should be built; modes with an empty `quantities` block work but the doc never shows a mode with no overrides.
- Which of two failed modes is reported (first in order?) is not documented; `closest_design` compares different units.
- A name collision between my quantity and a generated one (`P0`) gave an error showing internal names; the guide could warn that quantity names may not repeat.
