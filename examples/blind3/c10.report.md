# c10 report: 24 V to 5 V synchronous buck, component choice (problem 10)

Verdict on the brick as stated: **REFUTED** (all 180 designs fail in at least one mode, each at a concrete point; 9.8 s, 3G cap not hit).

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Vout = 5 V +-1% (given, tolerance), Vin in [18,36] (range), Iout in [0.5,3] (range); D = Vout/Vin, no `where` needed.
- fsw as a listed catalog (300/400/500 kHz, each +-10%); inductors L1..L5 with L +-20%, DCR, Isat, price; capacitors X1/X2/P1 with C +-20%, derating, ESR, price; count N = 1..4 as an `integers` catalog. 3*5*3*4 = 180 combinations, all enumerated (`report: all`).
- Ripple current dI = Vout(1-D)/(L fsw), Ipk = Iout + dI/2, dV = dI/(8 fsw Ceff) + dI*ESR_parallel, with Ceff = N*C*derating and ESR/N written in `define`. All tolerances (Vout, L, fsw, C) are live in every formula, so the "worst of L-20%, fsw-10%, C" is done by the stand, not by me.
- Five requirements as five `modes` (they have different operating points): dI<=1.2 A over the whole Vin range; Ipk<=Isat at Iout=3 A; dV<=30 mV at Vin=36, Iout=3; efficiency at 24 V and at 36 V (Iout=3). Mode overrides of Vin/Iout carry the "at Vin=36 V, Iout=3 A" wording exactly.
- Loss formula as given: Iout^2[RHS D + RLS(1-D) + 1.25 DCR] + 0.5 Vin Iout (tr+tf) fsw + Vin*4 mA + 0.1 W core + 50 mW*fsw/400 kHz. fsw tolerance therefore also enters the switching and gate loss (higher fsw = worse, found by the stand).
- Objective: minimize L.price + N*CAP.price (nominal, EUR).

## APPROXIMATED
1. Efficiency threshold rewritten by hand: eta >= eta0 <=> Pout*k - Ploss >= 0 with k = (1-eta0)/eta0 (2/23 for 92%, 1/9 for 90%). Exact rational, no rounding, but it is my algebra: the law `Em == Vout*Iout*kE - (...)` and the mode `define` `kE` are checked by nobody except me; the stand only checks `Em >= 0`. Safe (equivalent), but efficiency itself is never a quantity, and the margin is in watts, not percent. The reason: the requirement bound must not be a derived quantity and eta = Pout/(Pout+Ploss) is nonlinear.
2. DCR "+25% hot" written as the constant `5/4` inside the loss law (dimensionless, fine). The DCR has no tolerance in the problem, so none given. RDS(on) values are taken as "hot" already.
3. "Worst Vin" for ripple current and peak current: handled by giving Vin the whole range [18,36] in those modes (the stand takes the worst). For "peak current <= Isat at worst dI" the range covers it; I did not pin Vin=36. Same thing, not an approximation, just a reading.
4. Isat "where L drops by 20%" is treated as a plain current limit; the L drop near Isat is not modelled (the problem does not ask for it either).
5. Ceff: derating is a plain catalog number with no tolerance; ESR of parallel caps = ESR/N with no tolerance. As in the statement.
6. Nothing else changed. Units: mohm, uH, uF, kHz, ns, mA, mW all declared; no "units not checked" warning.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
1. Efficiency as a quantity with a percent requirement (see APPROXIMATED 1): need a margin form, and its sensitivity to the 1/(1+k) algebra is on me.
2. `minimal_conflicts` is not computed across modes, so the REFUTED verdict does not say WHICH requirements clash. I had to find it myself (below): the 1.2 A ripple limit against the 24 V efficiency / 36 V efficiency pair.
3. A REFUTED design shows only the first failing mode, not all failing modes; "which combinations are close" has to be read from `by`, which has different units per mode (A, V, W) and cannot be compared.
4. Nothing about the *question's second half* was lost: the lowest-cost design simply does not exist for the stated numbers.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
I wrote an independent float script (scratchpad `chk.py`): all 2^4 corners of Vout, fsw, L, C for each of the 180 designs, and Vin at the end points (and at 24/36 for efficiency).
- It gives 0 designs passing all five requirements - matches the stand. Pass counts per requirement alone: dI 60, peak 180, dV 123, eff24 72, eff36 108 of 180. Any four of five: only {peak, dV, eff24, eff36} passes (30 designs); every set containing the ripple-current requirement together with an efficiency requirement passes 0. So the conflict is dI<=1.2 A (needs L*fsw >= 3.62 V/A at the worst corner) vs the 24 V efficiency (loss budget 1.30 W at Pout 15 W; at 24 V switching loss alone is 0.36 W at 400 kHz).
- Hand check of one witness: L3, 500 kHz, X1 x1 fails ripple current: Vout 5.05, Vin 36, L=8 uH, f=450 kHz: 5.05*(1-5.05/36)/(8e-6*450e3) = 1.2060 A, overshoot 0.0060 A - matches the stand's 311/51840 = 0.005999.
- Stand's dV witness above (L=8, f=270, C=37.6, N=2, derating 0.45) recomputed by me: 0.0300061 V - matches.
- Scratch run (2.2 A): my corner script finds exactly the same 6 passing designs and the same cheapest (1.82 EUR); the stand's guaranteed dI range upper bound is 2.0165 ("ZTL, outer range") against my corner maximum 2.0100: the outer range is a bit wider than the truth (lost correlation, as the doc warns), which is sound but would give OPEN if the limit sat between 2.010 and 2.0165 (not tested).
- Not checked: the stand's ZTL proof internals; the E/mode ordering logic; behaviour of Vin between corners (I assumed the extremes at Vin=18/36 for dI, which is monotone; the stand does not assume it).

## DOC GAPS
- BRICKS.md does not say whether a `mode` may leave `"quantities": {}` empty (it worked) or that unused given quantities in a mode are fine (they are).
- `"report": "all"` is documented only in a bullet in section 8, not in the skeleton; it is a top-level key and it is not stated whether it is allowed together with `modes` (it is).
- For efficiency-type requirements (ratio of two quantities) the doc gives the ratiometric advice for Vout/Vin but nothing about "efficiency >= x%"; the margin form (Pout*k - Ploss >= 0) had to be found by me. A short example would help.
- Not said whether `define` can be replaced per mode when a name is not defined at top level (here kE exists at top and is overridden; I did not test the other way).
- `integers` with `unit: ""` and a catalog without `ground` worked; the doc does not say `ground` is optional for listed items.
