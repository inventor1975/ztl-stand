# c11 report: Reverberation correction of a seminar room (problem 11)

Final brick: `examples/blind3/c11.json` = the full physics, but panel counts restricted to multiples of 10 (0..120, sum <= 125) so it finishes in the time limit. Verdict: **REFUTED** (455 of 455 designs of the grid).

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- V = 345.6 m^3 +-2% (given, tolerance 0.02); occupancy 10..60 (given range, no unit).
- 6 octave bands = 6 `modes`; each mode overrides the band's alphas, person absorption, air m, and adds the T window of that band. "Holds in every band" = "EARNED in every mode".
- Existing-surface alphas: `range` value +-0.02 absolute (floor, ceiling, plaster, window, door - 5 independent quantities per band). Panel alphas: `value`, `tolerance 0.1` (relative, 3 quantities).
- Areas (96 / 96 / 120 plaster after windows+doors / 20 / 4 / 336 total), panel area 0.72, panels replace plaster: `(Sw - Span*Ntot)*aPl` in the Asurf law, panel absorption `Span*(NA.N*aA+NB.N*aB+NC.N*aC)`.
- Sabine/Eyring switch on the mean alpha of the SURFACES (not persons/air) as `if(Asurf/Stot <= 0.2, Sabine, Eyring)` with `ln` - written as stated, in one law `T == if(...)`.
- Air term 4*m*V uses the same toleranced V as the numerator (correlation kept inside the law).
- Constraint <=125 panels: `where` `NA.N+NB.N+NC.N <= 125`. Objective: panel price from givens, nominal (cheapest-first).
- Units on everything (m^2, s, s/m, 1/m, EUR); units were checked, no "not checked" line.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
Checked in python (own independent model, corner enumeration; T is monotone in every uncertain quantity except the Sabine->Eyring jump, which goes downward, so max T = all alphas low, V high, 10 persons; min T = all alphas high, V low, 60 persons):
- Witness (20,50,10) at 500 Hz: python gives T = 0.5999767979937 with Eyring (mean alpha 0.2279); stand 0.599976797994. Match to 12 digits. Witness (15,20,15) in the 5-grid run: python 0.9009326, stand 4347/4825 = 0.900933. Match.
- The stand's `closest_design` / overshoot is **not** the true gap: for (20,50,10) the worst corner (60 persons) gives T = 0.472 at 500 Hz (window 0.6..0.9) and 0.915 at the high side - a violation ~0.13 s, not 2e-5 s. The witness the stand reports is the first failing point it found, so "closest" ranks by witness overshoot. Misleading, not wrong.
- Full space (all 333 000 triples, my python, worst-corner): zero feasible. Best triple (0, 60, 6), worst relative violation 18.7%. On the step-10 grid the best is (0, 60, 10), 20.8%. So REFUTED for all designs is true under my model, but only the grid part is checked by the stand.
- Reason it is infeasible: with all tolerances OFF (occupancy 10..60 only) my python finds 1 248 feasible designs, cheapest (5, 57, 1) at 4 007 EUR; the stand EARNED exactly that design in the scratch run, ranges equal to python to 6 digits (e.g. 125 Hz upper 1.198738 vs window 1.2). So the tolerance box (+-0.02 on 5 surfaces, +-10% on panels, V +-2%) combined with 10..60 persons is what kills it; at 4000 Hz the two ends (T_hi 0.914 / T_lo 0.459 for the same design) are wider than the window 0.5..0.9 in ratio.
- Not checked: ZTL's internal certificates; stand's ranges for non-witness designs (only witnesses matched); the 255 unlisted designs.

## DOC GAPS
- `where` + objective: the output says `optimal: true, every cheaper design was refuted in some mode` when `where` had removed all cheaper designs (scratch EARNED run). "optimal" is relative to the where-filtered set; not stated.
- Speed: nothing in BRICKS.md says that the full product of catalogs is enumerated before `where` (3+ min for 2M combos) or that Eyring (`ln`) designs cost ~2x. No per-design cost guidance, so a "which integer counts" brick with 3 counters 0..125 is expressible but unusable.
- `closest_design`, `its_violating_point`, `overshoot` are for a witness, not the design's worst case; BRICKS.md says "overshoot ... at ITS WITNESS POINT" for the designs list, but section 8's table reads as if `closest_design` were the best design. For REFUTED with modes there is no `reasons`/`minimal_conflicts` (documented), so the user cannot see that 18% is the real gap.
- A leading unary minus: I wrote `0 - Stot*ln(...)` out of caution; the doc doesn't say whether `-x*ln(..)` parses.
- `if()` conditions on a DERIVED quantity (`Asurf/Stot <= 0.2`) work (doc example only shows given-side style `x > 1`); worth stating.
- Mode `quantities` can override a toleranced given with a `range` replaced by value/tolerance form and vice versa - worked (aFl range in all modes), not stated in 7b (it says value/tolerance/range overrides; fine, but the mix was not obvious).
