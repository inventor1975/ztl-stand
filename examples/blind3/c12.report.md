# c12 report: Grower feed for broilers, diet formulation (problem 12)

Files: `examples/blind3/c12.json` (final brick). Scratch (generator, LP, outputs): `/tmp/claude-1000/-media-vitaly-SSD-1000GB-Projects/f02e6bb8-b858-4561-80a5-37296edf7eb2/scratchpad/blind3/c12/` (gen.py builds the brick; lp.py/cands*.py find the candidate recipes; check.py is my own exact corner check; c12lit.json is a variant brick).

Design chosen: the recipe is NOT searched by the stand. One slot `MIX` takes one item from a listed catalog `recipes`; every item is a complete 10-ingredient recipe (fields xmz xwh xsb xsf xfm xol xls xdp xly xme, % of mix). The 12 candidate recipes were computed OUTSIDE by LP/MILP (scipy). The stand only judges them. Uncertain data are toleranced givens (31 of them); nutrient sums are laws; the requirement groups are six `modes` (see APPROXIMATED 1).

Result in one line: EARNED, cheapest verified robust recipe 337.074 EUR/t nominal (worst-case cost 360.90). Step-restricted (0.5%) problem: no robust recipe exists (shown by my LP, not by the stand).

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Ingredient table (ME, CP, Lys, Met+Cys, Ca, avP, fibre, price) as numbers in laws / givens.
- ME of the 6 energy-bearing ingredients +-2.5% (`tolerance 0.025`); Lys and Met+Cys +-3% relative (`0.03`); CP as absolute ranges [CP-1, CP+1]; maize moisture range 13..15 with scale S=(100-moist)/86 on ALL maize nutrient terms (ME, CP, Lys, MC, Ca, avP, fibre) via `define S`; prices of soybean +-15%, fish +-10% as toleranced givens.
- All nine nutrient requirements (ME>=3000, CP>=19.5, Lys>=1.10, Met+Cys>=0.85, Ca in [0.85,1.00], avP>=0.42, fibre<=5.0, and the Ca:avP window, see 1 below) judged "for every combination" of the uncertain data by ZTL, confirmed by the checker (ZTL exact at the corners).
- Inclusion limits (sunflower<=10, fish<=5, oil<=4, lysine<=0.4, methionine<=0.3, maize+wheat>=45) and the batch sum as exact `where` conditions on the catalog fields (stand-checked; excluded_by_where=0 because all my candidates satisfy them).
- Cheapest by table prices: `computed cost0` + `objective minimize cost0`, premix 0.5% x 1400 = 7 EUR/t included.
- Worst-case cost over the price ranges: derived `Cost` (with toleranced p_sb, p_fm) and a mode `price` with a dummy `Cost <= 100000`, so the proved range of Cost appears in `per_mode.price.guaranteed`.

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Modes used as a decomposition, not as physical modes (NOT checked by the stand).** With all 31 uncertain quantities in one judgement the checker refused: "31 toleranced quantities: 2^n corners too many for the checker" (all 9 designs OPEN). I made six modes (energy, protein, lysine, methcys, minerals_fibre, price); in each, every uncertain quantity that I judge irrelevant to that mode's requirements is overridden to its nominal (exact value). Sound only if my dependence reading is right (ME requirement does not depend on CP etc. - true by the laws, but the stand does not verify it). A mode override of a really relevant quantity would silently under-test. Only I checked this (and the check.py independent corner enumeration agrees with the stand's ranges).
2. **Optimisation is outside the stand.** The stand cannot choose continuous percentages. Candidate recipes come from my robust LP (worst-case coefficients, written by hand: ME/CP/Lys/MC at low end, maize x 85/86; Ca upper at S=87/86, avP lower etc.), rounded to 0.001%, with the maize share absorbing the remainder so the sum is exactly 99.2. The `optimal: true` / "every cheaper design was refuted" in the output is only relative to my 13 candidates, NOT a proof of global optimality. The global bound comes from my LP: robust optimum 336.770 EUR/t vs best EARNED rounded recipe 337.074 (gap 0.30 EUR/t, rounding + margin). Safe direction (EARNED is proved by the stand); optimality claim rests only on me.
3. **Ca:avP window by hand algebra.** Ratio 1.8..2.4 written as `D1 == Ca - 9/5*AP >= 0` and `D2 == 12/5*AP - Ca >= 0`. Equivalent only when avP>0 (also required avP>=0.42). A direct quotient would lose the shared-moisture correlation. Checked by me only; the stand also reported D1, D2 exact (ZTL, shared S handled exactly). I did not test the quotient form.
4. **Ingredients with CP = 0 (oil, limestone, DCP):** "+-1.0 absolute" taken as [0, 1] (clamped, CP cannot be negative). Literal [-1, 1] changes the answer: the 337.074 recipe then FAILS CP (19.4445 < 19.5; stand REFUTED it in the variant c12lit.json, by 0.0555) and the robust optimum becomes 337.27 -> stand EARNED 337.759 for a recipe I computed with the literal reading. I chose the clamped reading in the main brick; ambiguity of the problem text.
5. **Salt cost** is not given; I set it to 0 (only premix 7 EUR/t added). Constant offset, no ranking effect, but absolute EUR/t figures are low by 0.3% x salt price.
6. **0.5% step sum inconsistency.** 10 ingredients sum to 100 - 0.3 - 0.5 = 99.2%, not a multiple of 0.5. So "all in 0.5% steps" is impossible exactly; I let maize be the balancing ingredient (continuous), other nine in 0.5 steps. The "step" candidates carry a `grid` label only; the stand does not know or check "step".
7. **Step case is not answered by the stand.** Under the step rule lysine (<=0.4) and methionine (<=0.3) can only be 0. My LP: the continuous relaxation with lys=met=0 at full uncertainty is infeasible (status infeasible), hence NO robust 0.5%-step mix exists (fails mainly Met+Cys / ME / Ca low jointly). In the stand I only show 4 step candidates (nominal-feasible 384.04 EUR/t; ones for 25% and 50% uncertainty; and a "best-effort" one, max relaxation 7.9 units of my margin scale) - all REFUTED (Met+Cys). That shows these fail, it does not prove no step recipe exists; the proof is my LP (a relaxation argument, unchecked by the stand).
8. Moisture scaling of fibre/Ca/avP assumed ("all nutrient contents of maize"). Maize price not scaled.
9. Worst-case cost: not done with `"case":"worst"` (docs say not with modes). Instead read from the proved range of `Cost` (mode price; ZTL outer range, see TOOL OUTPUT).

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- **Choice of continuous quantities (or a large integer grid).** A slot picks one catalog item; a recipe with 10 free percentages is a product space of ~1e9+ items (step) or infinite (continuous). There is no "choose a number in [lo,hi]" variable and no LP-style design search. So the question "which mix is cheapest" was reduced to "judge these candidates".
- **Proof of global optimality / infeasibility.** `optimal` only covers listed candidates. "No step recipe exists" cannot be said in the format.
- **Per-requirement dependence for the checker.** The checker enumerates 2^(all toleranced quantities); a brick with many independent uncertainties on separate requirements (31 here, each requirement needs only 1-11) is OPEN. The mode-override trick works around it but is an unchecked claim by the author.
- A "step multiple of 0.5" condition: could not be a conditional `where` (it would exclude continuous items); only a label.
- Worst-case cost objective together with the modes workaround (docs: not combinable).
- Negative-CP / "absolute +-1 but not below zero" is expressible only by choosing the range by hand.
