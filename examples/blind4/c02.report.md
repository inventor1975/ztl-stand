# c02 report: Sacrificial-anode group on a coated tank (problem 2)

Final brick: `examples/blind4/c02.json` (copy of `work_c02/v2.json`). Verdict: EARNED, Mg-S-32 with n = 4, cost 4*(130+40) = 680 EUR, `optimal: true` (38 cheaper designs refuted). Full run 102 s, 39 designs judged, 3G/1 core.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Seven anode types as listed items (m, L, d, price, E_a, capacity); n = 1..60 as an integer catalog; two slots AN and CNT, so 420 combinations.
- E_a = nominal ± 0.05 V as a range per item.
- Dwight resistance with `ln(4L/d)`, the group factor `(1 + 0.15 ln n)/n` (n = 1 gives R1 automatically since ln 1 = 0), R_c = 0.2 ohm. All written as `define`s with `ln`, using real catalog quantities and units.
- Two seasons as two separate operating points (wet rho = 12, dry rho = 140 ohm m, exact givens), each solved by its own implicit law on the whole box of E_a, beta, I_p.
- beta in [0.12, 0.18], I_p in [0.012, 0.06] (protection mode), I_p = 0.030 exactly (life mode). Utilisation 0.9, install cost 40 EUR (a given in EUR).
- R1: `Esw`, `Esd` within [0.85, 1.18] V, for every box value (mode "protection").
- R2: `Life >= 262800` h with `Life == n*m*cap*Util/Iavg`, `Iavg == 0.5*Iw + 0.5*Id`, with the currents computed at I_p = 0.030 (mode "life").
- Objective `CNT.N*(AN.price + Cinst)`. Both currents are derived quantities, so the life law uses the real operating-point currents, not the demand.

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Capacity "8 % lower, no gain above nominal"** is a range [0.92c, c] (e.g. [1012, 1100]), computed by hand. Exact, not conservative or optimistic. Checked only by me (the stand just reads the range).
2. **30 years = 262800 h** (365-day year, 8760 h). The problem does not define a year. Choosing 365.25 d would raise the bound to 262980 h. I did not test that, and the margin for the winner (about 41.8 y) should absorb it. Checked only by me.
3. **Implicit law rearranged by hand.** The problem's form is `I*(R_group+R_c) = E_a - E_s` and `E_s = 0.85 + beta*log10(I/I_p)`. I eliminated I and made E_s the implicit unknown: `Ea - Es == Rt*Ip*pow(10, (Es - Vp)/beta)`, with `I == (Ea - Es)/Rt` afterwards. This is algebraically identical, with one root for E_s < E_a. Nobody but me checks the equivalence; the checker reads my rearranged law. My numeric cross-check (below) agrees to 4 digits.
   Why: the literal form (I implicit, `Esw == Ea - Iw*Rt` afterwards) came out OPEN (see TOOL OUTPUT). The reason is lost correlation: `Es = Ea - I*Rt` with the range of I treated independently of Ea gave [0.869, 1.216] V where the truth is within [0.85, 1.14]. With E_s implicit the range is tight. This is safe (sound), but the author has to know this trick.
4. **Search range** for E_s is [0.3, 1.9] V. If it were too narrow the design would be OPEN, never falsely EARNED, so safe. The stand checks root existence.
5. **"Worst case for life" is the whole box**, not a hand-picked corner (Ea +0.05, cap -8 %, "beta giving the larger current"). The box contains that corner. Because Life is monotone in cap and Ea, requiring the whole box is the same condition, and I do not have to say which beta is worse (it depends on whether I is above or below I_p). Equivalent, not stricter. The two modes are my encoding of "I_p range for R1, I_p = 0.030 for R2".
6. **Life reading of Iavg:** I = (Ea - Es)/Rt loses the Ea/Es correlation again, so the Life lower bound is a bit conservative (366 460 h = 41.8 y against about 42.4 y at my grid corners). Safe direction. Life is bounded "outside ZTL" (the stand's interval reading over ZTL's ranges), not by the judge.
7. **Shared beta/E_a across seasons:** in R1 each season is a separate requirement on the same box, which is exact. In R2 the correlation of beta and E_a between the two seasonal currents is kept only through interval ranges of Iw, Id (conservative).

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- Nothing in the physics was inexpressible once the implicit law was rearranged. The inexpressible items are of format kind:
- A mode cannot restate a catalog field (E_a high by 0.05, capacity low by 8 %) as a named "worst case". I had to rely on the box covering it.
- A season (a discrete scenario) has no native form: two seasons meant duplicating quantities, laws and implicit unknowns (Iw/Id, Esw/Esd, Rtw/Rtd). Modes cannot be used for them because R2 needs both seasons together in one number.
- Years are not a unit (`day` and `h` exist); I wrote 262800 h by hand.

## DOC GAPS
- BRICKS.md does not say that an implicit quantity should be the one whose range the requirement reads. When the requirement quantity is derived from the implicit one by another law, correlations with other uncertain parameters are lost and the result goes OPEN. I found this only by running. A short note "make the quantity you bound the implicit unknown" would have saved a 15-minute failed run.
- The stand prints nothing until it finishes (no progress), and a timeout kill gives no partial result. A hint about per-design cost (about 20 s with one implicit quantity per season, in two modes) would help with sizing.
- `pow(10, expr)` with a non-constant exponent is not mentioned as allowed; it worked.
- The text says the `search` interval must not make a log argument invalid, but gives no advice about the E_a - E_s > 0 domain.
- `optimal: true` is printed even when I limit the design space with `where` (in my single-design test 419 combinations were excluded and it still said "every cheaper design was refuted"). The note should mention exclusions.
- Not said whether `ln(CNT.N)` inside a `define` is allowed with an integer-catalog quantity; it worked.
- Units: `ohm*m` and `A*h/kg` worked; "year" is not available and the doc doesn't say so.
