## APPROXIMATED
1. **h_s is continuous in the problem; I made it a grid** (`integers` N, h = N * 0.01 m). The continuous format (LP) needs requirements affine in the amount; here S depends on h through log10 and multiplies U (derived, nonlinear), so it does not apply. Not conservative for the cost: the grid optimum 0.18 m (150 075 EUR) is above the true continuous optimum (my own bisection: h = 0.1762 m, 149 647 EUR, 0.3% lower). Feasibility is safe (any EARNED grid point is a true design). Only I checked the size of the discretisation loss, the stand cannot.
2. **Window of the grid in the final brick: N = 5..30, i.e. h = 0.05..0.30 m**, not 0..2.5 m. Chosen to keep the run at 3.5 min. The window comes from my earlier full-range run (step 0.05, h 0..2.5, same stand, EARNED at 0.20 m, 2 min 9 s), so the final "optimal: true" holds only inside the window; outside it I rely on the earlier coarse full-range run. Not checked by the stand in a single run.
3. **c_v given in m2/day** (unit list has no year): 1.4/365 = 7/1825, 3.0/365 = 3/365, 1.8/365 = 9/1825, 4.5/365 = 9/730 as exact fractions, t in days. Exact, checked only by me.
4. **Drain count is fractional** (area/(0.866 s^2), no ceiling), as the problem states "drains per m2".
5. Zone coupling of uncertainties: I treat zone 1 and zone 2 uncertain parameters as independent. If c_h/c_v etc. were one shared value across zones, the stand's box is larger than the real one (conservative). Statement says "for both zones ... anywhere in" which is ambiguous.
6. 1/5 for 0.2 and 105/100 for 1.05, 866/1000 for 0.866: exact, no change.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- A continuous choice entering through a nonlinear (log10) law and a product with a derived quantity: no way to say "h_s any real in [0, 2.5]"; grid only. Largest loss of fidelity: the optimum is a discrete-grid optimum, 0.3% above the real one.
- A real-valued year unit (had to convert to days).
- The same surcharge/drain-type constraint "same on the whole job" was expressible (one slot each, shared) and was; nothing else missing.
- "Optimal over h_s continuous" or an optimality statement across a window vs the full range in one run: not in the format; cost forced the window.
