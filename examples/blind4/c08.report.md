## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Cell table (8 cells, V_r / C / ESR / price), balancing 0.50 EUR as a given in the objective `NS.N*NP.N*(CELL.price + Cbal)`.
- n_s in 6..15 (integers catalog), n_p integer (integers 1..16), `where NS.N*NP.N <= 100`.
- Ambient T as a given range [-20, 65] C; the piecewise-linear ESR multiplier written as five nested `if(...)` pieces
  with the six table points (all 6 knots reproduced), evaluated by the stand over the whole T box.
- EOL: ESR = 2.0 x nominal x multiplier(T); capacitance factor as a range [0.72, 0.80] (0.72 = 0.80 x 0.9 as stated).
- R_bank = n_s ESR / n_p, C_bank = n_p C / n_s (as defines, with the EOL factors inside).
- Discriminant of the terminal-voltage root: requirement `dis = Vsmin^2 - 4 R P_in >= 0` ("load cannot be supplied").
- R1: `Vpk = 26.4/n_s*1.06 <= V_r`. R2: hold >= 8 s at EOL, 21.6 V start, all ambients. R3: life from the stated
  law with `pow(2, ...)`, the 60/30/10 % profile at 25/45/60 C, 24 V float x 1.03/n_s, life >= 131400 h.
- Units checked ("every law's dimensions agree").

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Hold time by a hand-derived closed form (NOT conservative-by-construction; checked only by me).** The format has no ODE
   and the implicit-law feature finds roots, not integrals. I integrated dt = C v_t dv_c / P_in with
   v_t = (v+sqrt(v^2-4RP))/2 from v_c = 21.6 V down to v_c1 = 10 + RP/10 (where v_t = 10 V):
   thold = C/P*((v0^2-v1^2)/4 + (v0 s0 - v1 s1 - 4RP ln((v0+s0)/(v1+s1)))/4). The stand only evaluates this formula; it
   cannot check that it IS the discharge time. I checked it against an Euler ODE simulation (dt 0.2 ms) at 2743 corner
   cases: max difference 0.27 ms. Exact algebra, but only I vouch for it.
2. **Simplified s1 and v1+s1 by hand.** s1 = sqrt(v1^2 - 4RP) = Vmin - RP/Vmin and v1+s1 = 2 Vmin. This is valid only
   when RP <= Vmin^2 (v_t = 10 V is the UPPER root). My first version kept the sqrt: 178 designs came out OPEN
   ("cannot bound ['thold']", 98 of them truly passing) because interval arithmetic loses the correlation between
   the occurrences of R_bank and v1^2-4RP goes negative. The simplification fixed that.
3. **Added requirement `mB = Vmin^2 - R P_in >= 0` (not in the problem).** It makes the simplification valid. It could wrongly
   REFUTE a design with 100 < RP <= 116 that would survive 8 s until collapse. By my own check (ODE with collapse
   handling, worst corner, all 800 designs) no design with RP > 100 survives 8 s, so no verdict changes. Who checks:
   only me (`ref.py`); the stand judges mB as an ordinary requirement.
4. **`s0 = sqrt(max(Vsmin^2 - 4RP, 0))`.** Without the clamp a negative discriminant in the box makes thold unboundable
   -> OPEN instead of REFUTED. With it, thold is garbage (not 0 s) in that regime, but `dis >= 0` is a separate
   requirement and refutes. Side effect: the reported "by" for the hold requirement on such designs (e.g. 7.06 s for
   S5 10x1) is an artifact; the physical hold there is 0 s. Verdict right, number meaningless.
5. **Hold only at the minimum supply (21.6 V) and R1 only at 26.4 V**, as two exact givens (`Vsmin`, `Vsmax`), rather than a
   supply range. This is exactly what R1/R2 say; the data "supply anywhere in 21.6-26.4" is therefore not a box.
6. **Load collapse timing not modelled separately:** hold ends at v_t = 10 V (monotone in v_c); disc < 0 at the start is
   a failure; collapse mid-discharge in the RP>Vmin^2 regime is covered by approximation 3.
7. n_p upper bound 16 is derived by me (n_s >= 6, n_s n_p <= 100); exact, not an approximation.
8. Rationals written by hand in the multiplier (2/25, 7/100, 7/250, 1/200, 1/400, 12/5, 17/10): exact, but my transcription.
9. R3 / R1 contain no uncertainty (all exact), so the stand's "tolerance" machinery does nothing there; fine, but
   the problem's "-10 % tolerance" on capacitance is only an interval [0.72, 0.80] I chose (worst end is 0.72 either way).

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- A time-domain ODE / integral of a discharge (hence approximation 1). No `integral`, no simulation law.
- A piecewise-linear table as data (had to hand-write slopes and knots in nested `if`); no `interp`.
- "Hold fails when v_t < 10 V before 8 s, or the discriminant goes negative at any moment during the discharge" as a
  trajectory property: only the start discriminant and the branch condition are checked.
- `sin/cos` not needed here. The REFUTED overshoot for a design whose load is unsuppliable cannot be stated as 0 s hold.

## DOC GAPS
- BRICKS.md does not warn that repeated occurrences of one toleranced define inside a nonlinear formula are read as
  independent by the interval reading: sqrt of an expression that is positive in truth becomes "negative" and the
  design goes OPEN with only `cannot bound ['thold']` (no mention of the domain). Needed hand algebra to dodge.
- `max(x, 0)` inside `sqrt` as the way to keep a design REFUTED rather than OPEN is undocumented (I guessed; it worked).
- The "guaranteed" range is described as proved, but for certificates its lower end is just the requirement bound
  (here exactly 8) and the upper end can be 2x loose (31.95 vs 17.17); not stated.
- No statement whether decimals ("0.08") are allowed inside law text; I used fractions.
- Comparisons in `if` with a quantity that has a unit (`T < -10`): not stated whether bare numbers are accepted; I
  avoided it by `u = T/Tu` with `Tu = 1 C`. This workaround is not in the doc.
- `designs_tried: 319` vs 800 after where: the doc does not say it counts only designs up to the first EARNED.
- `valid` on laws: not used; unclear if it accepts derived quantities (the doc example uses givens only).
