# c10 report: Micro-hydro two-section penstock and turbine (problem 10)

Files: `c10.json` (final brick, generated by `work_c10/gen.py`), my float reference `work_c10/ref.py`, outputs in `work_c10/`.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Catalog: 14 pipes (D, C, allowable head, EUR/m) in one catalog used by two slots UP and LO; 5 turbines (K, six efficiency points, shut-down fraction, price). All numbers exact, as in the table. 14*14*5 = 980 designs.
- Hazen-Williams loss per section with the given constants, `10.67*L*Q^1.852/(C^1.852*D^4.87)`; H_net(Q) = H_g - h_f,upper - h_f,lower, evaluated at the flow actually used (Q_i), not only at Q_full.
- The circular dependence Q_full = K sqrt(H_net(Q_full)) as a genuine implicit law (`search` on QF), solved by the stand for each design.
- Flow used `Q_i = min(f*Qr_i, QF)`, `phi_i = Q_i/QF`, six flow-duration points with their fractions, annual energy = sum fraction*8760 h*P_i.
- Piecewise-linear efficiency with `if()` chain over the six listed fractions; zero (shut down) below 0.2, and below 0.4 for T5. Electric power with 0.93, 9.81, rho=1000.
- R1 (energy >= 520 MWh at factor 0.85) and R2 (peak <= 125 kW at 1.15), R3 (velocities <= 3.5 in both sections at QF, factor 1.15) as two modes ("low river 0.85", "high river 1.15"). R4 (pressure: allowable head >= 1.3 x 40 and 1.3 x 85) as top-level requirements on margin quantities MU, ML.
- Objective: UP.price*240 + LO.price*180 + TB.price, minimise.

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Turbine K carried as K2 = K^2** (catalog field, exact rational, e.g. 0.0225^2 = 81/160000), because the unit parser takes integer powers only (m^2.5/s impossible) and I wanted no sqrt of a possibly negative head. Law is `QF*QF == K2*(HG - hf_u(QF) - hf_l(QF))`. Safe: exact algebra, one root on QF>0. Checked by the stand (it solves the law as written); the squaring is mine.
2. **Peak power** = `max` of the six P_i (nested `max`), then one requirement. Equivalent to "peak". Stand-checked.
3. **R3 uses QF** (literal "full turbine flow") not the largest Q_i at factor 1.15. For T4 (QF can approach 0.32 m3/s, about the largest flow 1.15*0.28 = 0.322) this is slightly conservative; not safe to call it "the" intent, only the literal reading. Nobody checks the intent.
4. **T5 efficiency below 0.4**: table says 0.0/0.0 "cannot run"; I put e02=e03=0.62 (dummy, never used because phimin=0.4 forces eta=0 below). Equivalent; only I checked it, and T5 never came near being relevant (all T5 designs refuted).
5. **Hazen-Williams unit constant**: the 10.67 is dimensioned; I made Q and D dimensionless by dividing by given unit quantities Q0 = 1 m3/s and D0 = 1 m (needed because non-integer `pow` needs dimensionless arguments). Equivalent numerically; units of the result (m) are checked by the stand, the equivalence only by me.
6. **Flow factor as point values per mode** (0.85 in R1's mode, 1.15 in R2/R3's), as the problem states the requirements "at the factor". The common-factor uncertainty is not a `range` in the brick; so nothing is proved for factor values between. Not conservative in general (energy/peak could in principle be non-monotone in the factor); the problem's own wording asks only for the two ends.
7. First run had my search bound QF in [0.005, 0.3]; T4 (K=0.042) has QF up to ~0.32, so three cheaper T4 designs came out OPEN ("no root in the search range"). Widened to [0.005, 0.45] (> K*sqrt(85) = 0.387 for T4, the absolute maximum). My error; the stand's behaviour (OPEN with the reason) was right.
8. No `valid` condition for H_net > 0 was written; not needed since at Q <= QF, H_net >= QF^2/K2 > 0 by the root.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- Fractional unit power (`m^2.5/s`) for K.
- "Common factor in [0.85, 1.15], requirement evaluated at one end": expressible only as two point-valued modes, not as one world where the requirement is attached to a chosen value of a toleranced quantity.
- A cross-mode sum or "peak over all modes" is not needed here, but annual energy over flow bins could not use modes (one mode per flow would lose the sum) -> I put the six flows as six parallel copies of the quantity chain (6 x 5 derived quantities plus 6 laws each) generated by a script. Verbose, error-prone by hand.
- Piecewise-linear curves as data: no table/`interp` function; efficiency curve hand-written as a 5-deep `if` chain with slopes in `define`.
- Nothing needed to be cut otherwise.

## DOC GAPS

- Units: says "integer powers" but does not say plainly that fractional powers (m^2.5) fail; I did not test, avoided.
- No guidance on how big `search` must be: a too-small bound gives OPEN "no root" which reads like a design property rather than my bound; the OPEN message could say "root not in [a,b]".
- Not stated whether `if` can compare a derived quantity with a catalog field (`PH1 < TB.phimin`); it worked.
- Not stated that `min`/`max` of derived quantities in a definition are accepted with an implicit-law quantity as argument; it worked.
- `ties` block does not say whether the tie meets the hard requirements that `where`/R4 would exclude.
