# c12 report: Pension liability matching with bonds under default and call scenarios (problem 12)

Files: `c12.json` (final brick, with R4; generated by `work_c12/gen.py`), `work_c12/c12_noR4.json` (same, R4 dropped),
variants `work_c12/v_*.json` with outputs `out_*.txt`, my own float reference `work_c12/ref.py` and `mis.py` (scipy/HiGHS LP and MILP).

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- 14 bonds as 14 continuous slots N1..N14 (lots 0..100), all coupons, maturities, prices, call years, ratings as exact givens (`c_B8 = 13/200`, `p_B8 = 26/25`, ...).
- Money in EUR exact (face 10000 EUR per lot, liabilities 400000 ... 650000 EUR, cost of a lot = price/100 x face).
- Three scenarios as three modes: Base (no overrides), Stress (`w_B6, w_B8, w_B11 = 1/2`: the BBB payment share), Fall (`e_B8 = e_B11 = 3`: last paying year = call year).
  Cumulative cash of a lot through year t is a `define`: `w*face*(c*min(t,e) + if(e <= t, 1, 0))` (coupons at the end of years 1..e, principal in year e).
- R1: six derived cumulative surpluses S_t = cumulative receipts - cumulative liabilities, each `>= 0` in every mode (no borrowing, 0 % carry). Top-level requirements, so they apply in all three modes.
- R2: derived `M2 = 3/10*C - CB >= 0` (C total cost, CB BBB cost), in every mode.
- R3: the liability duration Dl is computed INSIDE the brick (`define` from the given liabilities and the 1.04^-t discount factors, exact rationals). The portfolio duration window is
  written as two affine derived quantities DA = sum_t (t - Dl - 1/4) PV_t <= 0 and DB = sum_t (Dl - 1/4 - t) PV_t <= 0, in the Base mode only.
- R4: `C <= 2300000` EUR. Objective: total purchase cost, minimise.
- "If R4 dropped": a second brick without the R4 line.

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Integer lots became continuous amounts (0..100, any real).** The format has two ways to choose a number: integers catalog (enumerated, product of sizes = 101^14, impossible) or continuous (LP). I chose the LP. Result: the stand answers the LP RELAXATION. For REFUTED this is safe (the relaxation infeasible => the integer problem infeasible; the Farkas proof is ZTL's). For the minimum cost it is NOT the asked answer: the stand gives 2306284 EUR as a proved lower bound, integers cost more (2308670 by my MILP). Checked by me only (HiGHS MILP, float).
2. **Integer optimum found outside the stand** (scipy MILP, `work_c12/ref.py`): lots 32,25,0,38,24,0,45,0,0,60,0,3,0,2 for B1..B14 at 2308.67 kEUR. I then put this design into a brick as fixed one-value integer catalogs (`v_int_noR4.json`) and the stand EARNED it in all three modes. So feasibility of the integer design is the stand's; that it is the CHEAPEST integer design is only my MILP. The stand's `optimal: true` on that brick is vacuous (one design).
3. **Money in EUR, not kEUR**: the unit parser rejects `kEUR` ("unit 'kEUR' not known"). All numbers scaled by 1000 by me (safe, exact).
4. **Duration inequality linearised by hand**: |D - Dl| <= 1/4 turned into sum PV_t (t - Dl -+ 1/4) <=0 (multiply by sum PV_t > 0). Exact algebra, but mine; no one checks that the two forms are equivalent. The stand does check the arithmetic of DA/DB.
5. **Conflict set not asked from the stand**: with 18+ requirements `minimal_conflicts` was not produced (continuous mode prints only the Farkas verdict, no multipliers). I found the conflict by building sub-bricks (see TOOL OUTPUT); the sub-brick choice is mine.
6. R2 stated at purchase cost, same in all modes (not scenario value) - the reading of "total purchase cost".

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- **Integer lots in a large catalog**: no integer-variable LP/MILP route. 14 slots of 0..100 is not enumerable, and the continuous mode does not accept integrality. So the true integer minimum cannot be asked of the stand, only verified for a given design (one-value integer slots) and lower-bounded by the relaxation.
- A ratio requirement (duration) directly: had to be linearised by hand.
- `kEUR` as a unit.
- Asking "which requirements conflict" in continuous mode: no `minimal_conflicts` there; no printed Farkas multipliers (only the weighted sum -3159.01).

## TOOL OUTPUT

- `c12_noR4.json`: **EARNED** (continuous), 3 min 44 s. Mix (lots): B1 31.59, B2 27.22, B4 38.04, B5 24.37, B7 45.10, B10 61.90, others 0. Value 2306284.19 EUR, `lower_bound` 2306283.96 EUR (proved from LP duals by ZTL), `gap` 0.2306 EUR, `optimal: false` (only because of that 0.23 EUR gap). Margins of S_t at the mix are 0.04..0.27 EUR (the stand's mix sits just inside).
- Integer design 32,25,0,38,24,0,45,0,0,60,0,3,0,2 (`v_int_noR4.json`): EARNED, 4 min 8 s, cost 2308670 EUR; S1..S6 = 2400, 8400, 6900, 2100, 100, 100 EUR in all three modes. With R4 added (`v_int_R4.json`): REFUTED, 10 s, "fails R4 by 8670 EUR".
- Which requirements conflict (my sub-bricks):
  - `v_stress_R4.json` (only the Stress mode, R1 cover + R4): REFUTED with Farkas, 4.7 s. So the pair {R1 cover in the Stress scenario, R4 budget} already conflicts alone; at the LP level the Stress-scenario cover alone costs 2306284 > 2300000. R2 and R3 are not part of the conflict (slack at the optimum), Base and Fall alone cost only 2220 and 2247 kEUR (LP).
  - `v_nostress_cover_R4.json` (Base + Fall cover, R4, no R2/R3): EARNED, 2 min 26 s.
  - `v_nostress_int_R4.json` (Base + Fall + R2 + R3 + R4, a fixed integer design 31,26,1,35,0,67,3,0,0,58,0,0,0,4): EARNED, 2 min 43 s, cost 2271690 EUR.
- **A wrong-looking verdict:** `v_nostress_R4.json` and `v_nostress_noR4.json` (Base + Fall + R2 + R3, with or without R4) returned REFUTED with the text "no mix the solver found was earned" (16 s) although the integer design just above, which is a point of that same continuous box, was EARNED by the stand itself. At the LP vertex R2 and the cover constraints are binding at zero margin (my check: R2 margin 3e-13; cover slacks 0), and the stand's rational mix presumably misses by rounding. So REFUTED without "Farkas" does not mean "no mix exists", but the word REFUTED says so. I did not see the failing point (no witness printed).
- No crash, no memory or time kill. Peak not measured.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
Checked with my own float LP/MILP (HiGHS) from the problem table, independent of the brick:
- Dl = 3.666846 years at 4 %.
- LP minimum cost with all constraints but R4: 2306.28396 kEUR, matches the stand's `lower_bound` 2306283.9604539 EUR to 10 digits, and the mix (31.59, 27.22, 0, 38.04, 24.37, 0, 45.10, 0, 0, 61.90, 0...) matches. The binding scenario is Stress alone (LP 2306.284; Base alone 2220.186; Fall alone 2247.291). With R4 = 2300 the LP is infeasible, consistent with REFUTED. Integer min 2308.67 > 2300 too, so the integer problem is infeasible as well.
- Integer design hand check of S1: 32 x 10200 + 25 x 300 + 38 x 350 + 24 x 300 + 45 x 400 + 60 x 500 = 402400 EUR, less 400000 = 2400 EUR, as the stand printed. Float recomputation of all S_t in 3 scenarios equals the stand's guaranteed values; duration of the portfolio 3.65896 vs Dl 3.66685 (diff -0.0079, inside +-0.25); DA = -590606 as stand printed; BBB share 0 %; cost 2308.67 kEUR.
- Not checked: the integer minimality (only HiGHS says 2308.67, a float MILP); the Farkas weights (not shown); the cause of the false REFUTED in the no-Stress variants (my guess: zero-margin vertex); that the stand's lower bound rests on the same coefficients (I only saw the number agree).
- Answer to the problem: with R4 no portfolio (even fractional) exists; the cheapest portfolio without R4 costs 2308670 EUR (integers; 2306284 for the relaxation, proved lower bound). Conflict: R1 in the Stress scenario vs R4.

## DOC GAPS
- BRICKS.md lists currencies but not that `k` prefix does not combine with them (`kEUR` is a BRICK ERROR). The prefix list is stated "p n u m c k M G" for units in general.
- The continuous section does not say that integer-valued amounts are not supported, nor that REFUTED without "Farkas" may be a false negative (solver's mix failed). The text "no mix the solver found was earned" under verdict REFUTED contradicts the paragraph "Without the proof it stays OPEN".
- It does not say that `minimal_conflicts` and the Farkas weights are absent in continuous mode.
- `if`, `min` inside a `define` used as an LP coefficient in the continuous mode worked (not stated).
- Duration of an EARNED run: continuous mode with 18 requirements x 3 modes took 2.5-4 min; REFUTED by Farkas takes 5-12 s. Not documented.
- `optimal: true` for a brick with one single design (fixed integer slots) is vacuous; the doc does not warn.
