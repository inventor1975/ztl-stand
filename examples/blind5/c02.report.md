# c02 report: Distribution transformer with optional capacitor bank, 30-year loss evaluation (problem 2)

Brick: `examples/blind5/c02.json` (generated by `work_c02/gen.py`; independent check in `work_c02/check.py`).
Verdict: EARNED. Design: amorphous class, 1000 kVA (uk 6 %, P0 340 W, Pk 9800 W, 14.6 kEUR, 2820 kg) + bank C150 (1.7 kEUR). Worst-case lifetime cost: proved at most 141.51 kEUR, reached at least 131.76 kEUR (checker corners). `optimal: true`.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- The 18 rating x class combinations (three listed catalogs S/L/A with Sn, uk, P0, Pk, price, mass) and the four banks (none/C100/C150/C200, Qc and price) as two slots: 72 designs.
- The four seasons' P and pf as separate toleranced givens (independent ranges), hours per season, summer-night P and pf, tariff 0.09..0.14 EUR/kWh as a range. Independence of all ranges is the stand's default.
- Q = P*sqrt(1-pf^2)/pf (that is P tan(acos pf), identity), S = sqrt(P^2+(Q-Qc)^2) with the absolute-value behaviour (squared, so no abs needed).
- Loss per season P0 + Pk (S/Sn)^2 + 2.5 W per kvar * Qc; year-1 annual loss energy as sum hours*W (R1).
- Year-30 S (R2) and year-30 delta_U (R3) in every season: four requirements each, not aggregated. R4 (summer night, year 1, signed q), R5 (mass <= 3000 kg), all as requirements (no `where` pre-filter, so refutation reasons are real).
- r = Pk/(1000 Sn), x = sqrt(uk^2 - r^2), delta_U = p r + q x, as `define`s and laws; unit `%` used for uk and delta_U (stand does the 100 factor; bound written 3.8 and -0.8 in %).
- Objective: minimize lifetime cost in the WORST case (`"case": "worst"`), as the problem says "rank by worst-case lifetime cost".

## APPROXIMATED
1. The 30-year present value is NOT 30 yearly laws. By hand algebra: S_y^2 = g^2(P^2+Q^2) - 2 g Q Qc + Qc^2 with g = 1.012^(y-1); summing over y with weight 1/1.03^y gives A2*(P^2+Q^2) - 2*A1*Q*Qc + A0*Qc^2 + closed-form geometric sums A_k = (1 - q^30)/(1-q)/1.03, q = 1.012^k/1.03 (k=0,1,2), written as `define`s with `pow(q,30)`. Safe in principle (exact identity), but the algebra is MINE: the stand judges the formula I gave it, not the yearly sum. Checked only by me (check.py does the explicit 30-year loop; the earned design's worst cost matches to 6 digits: 131.759117 kEUR).
2. kvar and kVA are written in `kW` (the unit list has no var/VA). Bank loss 2.5 W/kvar is a given `kb` = 2.5 `W/kW`. Dimensionally consistent, but the stand cannot tell active from reactive power. Not checked by anyone.
3. Year-30 growth g30 = 1.012^29 as `pow(1.012,29)` (a ~1e-14 bracket, not exact). Safe (sound bracket); ZTL/checker treat it.
4. "Worst-case lifetime cost" = the stand's `case: worst` bound: an upper bound (141.51) looser than the true worst (131.76, ~7 %). The stand says optimal because every other design was refuted; with more earned designs the looseness could have left "optimal" unclaimed.
5. Seasons are separate named givens and laws (4x copies via a generator script), not modes: because a lifetime total over seasons is needed anyway. No safety issue; just verbose.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- A sum over years / seasons as a primitive (had to hand-reduce the 30-year sum to three closed-form constants).
- Units VA / var; reactive vs apparent vs active power distinction.
- `max over seasons` as one requirement was not tried; 11 requirements > 8, so `minimal_conflicts` would not be computed (it was not needed here, verdict EARNED).
- Nothing about the problem itself was left unsaid.

## TOOL OUTPUT
- Run: 18 s wall (heavy -m 3G --cores 1), no kill, no crash. 72 designs tried, 64 failed at nominal point (cheap), 8 judged, 0 undecided; 71 REFUTED, 1 EARNED. `units: checked`.
- `how` for every derived quantity is "ZTL, interval reading of the definition (sound, may be wide)" plus checker bounds; nothing proved with certificates (none needed).
- First run showed objective value 141514 with no unit (read as EUR); added `"unit": "kEUR"` to the objective, then the top value reads 141.5148 kEUR. But the per-design `objective_worst.proved_at_most` / `reached_at_least` inside `designs` still print 141514.83 / 131759.12 (not in kEUR) -- inconsistent with the top-level value.
- The `by` of a REFUTED design is the witness overshoot, not the worst; e.g. L-1000 + C150 fails R1 "by 31.7 kWh" while the true worst case overshoots 32000 by 1250.6 kWh (my corner check). Documented, but easy to misread.
- Key results: earned design guaranteed E1 in [22666.5, 31547.8] kWh (limit 32000), dU30 winter up to 3.7078 % (limit 3.8), S30 max 977.4 of 1050, night dU in [-0.618, -0.454] % (limit -0.8), mass 2820.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
Checked with check.py (independent Python: 41x41 grid per season, explicit 30-year yearly loop, floats, no closed forms):
- Exactly one design passes all five requirements, the same one (A, 1000 kVA, C150). All 71 other designs fail at least one requirement in my check too. Cost ordering: the cheaper designs fail R5 (mass) or R1 (losses) or R3/R4.
- Matches: worst-case lifetime cost 131.759 kEUR (stand reached_at_least 131.759117); E1 max 31547.8 (stand 31547.8); dU30 winter max 3.7078 (stand 3.70778); S30 ratio, dUn range -0.618 (stand -0.618371). Corner check of L-1000+C150 gives R1 overshoot 1250.6 kWh (stand witness only 31.7, a smaller corner) -- both refuted.
- Not checked: the "proved_at_most" 141.51 as a bound beyond seeing it is above the true 131.76; the stand's looseness reasons; refutation reasons for each design individually beyond "some requirement fails". The yearly growth of pf "unchanged" and the tariff "constant" were encoded as read.
- Tightest margins of the winner: R1 (452 kWh), R3 winter (0.092 %), R4 (0.18 %).
