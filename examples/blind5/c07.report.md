# c07 report: shielding of a radiography room wall (problem 7)

Final brick: examples/blind5/c07.json (112 combinations, 3 slots with listed items of thickness t in mm, 16 toleranced givens, 3 derived-quantity requirements, price objective). Scratch: examples/blind5/work_c07/ (run1 = unit error, run2 = 20-quantity OPEN, run3 = final, chk.py = my own check).

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- All 112 combinations as three listed-item catalogs (lead 7, barite 4, concrete 4), thickness 0 as an ordinary item (no `optional` needed).
- The 12 HVLs as `value` + `tolerance 0.1`, independent per material and exam type (12 separate quantities). Their use as `x/HVL` inside `define` (S_j), `B_j == pow(2, 0 - S_j)`, so B_j is the exact transmission law, layers in series.
- H = 1.14*U*T*d1^2/d^2 * sum NK_j B_j with U=0.25, T=1.0, d=2.2 m as givens with units; d1 = 1 m reference distance.
- Mass and thickness as laws from per-mm densities (givens, kg/(m^2*mm)) and the thicknesses; R1/R2/R3 as `<=` requirements 0.005 / 55 / 60. Price as objective `Area*(pPb*PB.t + ...)` with prices in EUR/(m^2*mm), area 14 m^2 a given.
- "Worst case = every uncertain quantity independently" is exactly the stand's for-all semantics.

## APPROXIMATED
1. N_j and K_j merged into one given NK_j = N_j*K_j with range [N0*K0, N1*K1] (154..234, 72..120, 200..360, 150..270). Reason: with 20 toleranced quantities the checker refused (see TOOL OUTPUT); with 16 it worked. This is EXACT, not conservative: N_j and K_j enter H only as a product, they are independent positive ranges, so the product's range is exactly that interval and every value in it is reachable. Checked only by me (hand algebra; the stand cannot see it). Cost: the report names NK_chest instead of N and K separately.
2. Units: mGy and mSv are not known to the stand. I used mJ/kg (Gy = Sv = J/kg dimensionally) for K, NK, H; the factor 1.14 Sv/Gy is a dimensionless given. The requirement "mSv per week" is "H in mJ/kg", the week is implicit in the N_j. Safe, but the unit check cannot catch a mix-up between kerma and dose, nor a missing per-week.
3. `K at 1 m` handled with a given d1 = 1 m so that d1^2/d^2 is dimensionless: faithful, only a device.
4. R1 "for every admissible value" -> requirement H <= 0.005, with units mJ/kg. No change in meaning.
Nothing was rounded; decimals are exact fractions.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- Units Gy, Sv, mGy, mSv, week (workaround above).
- Nothing structural in this problem was missing. The format does cope with a for-all over 16 independent toleranced quantities in a monotone function, but only when the CHECKER can enumerate corners: 20 toleranced quantities was too many (OPEN), 16 passed. The limit is on the checker, not expressible in the brick; I had to find the merge by hand. Merging N_j*K_j is available here only because the product is exact; a case where the independent quantities could not be merged would stay OPEN with no way to say so in the brick.
- A cost "per m2 times area" was fine; no thickness-dependent cross terms needed.

## TOOL OUTPUT
- Run 1 (20 toleranced givens: 8 N/K + 12 HVL): after unit fix, 0.4 s, verdict OPEN: 111 designs refuted at nominal, 1 undecided: `the checker could not evaluate it: 20 toleranced quantities in one group of coupled laws: 2^n corners too many for the checker` (design lead 3 mm, no barite, no concrete). Before that, a first run gave `BRICK ERROR: UnitError: unit 'mGy' not known`.
- Run 3 (final, 16 toleranced; work_c07/run3.txt): verdict REFUTED: "every design in the catalog violates a requirement at a concrete point". designs_count REFUTED 112, undecided 0, `failed_at_nominal` 111, judged 1. closest_design: lead 3 mm, barite 0, concrete 0. Violating point (a mixed corner: Pb/Ba/Co HVLs at +-10% variously, NK at their minima for ext/chest/spine/pelvis) gives H = 0.0051105 mSv/week > 0.005, overshoot 0.00011. spread_over_tolerances for that design: H from 0.0012592 to 0.0103768. minimal_conflicts: exactly one set {R1, R2} (dose and mass), `undecided_sets: []`. R3 (thickness) appears in no conflict set.
- Wall time of the final run: real 17m21s, user 17m20s (about 1041 s) although invoked under `timeout 900`. The run was not killed (the output is complete); I did not investigate why the 900 s limit did not apply. Progress lines showed ~100-110 s per 112-design pass, and several passes (one per subset of requirements for minimal_conflicts). The 3 G memory cap was not hit. The machine was shared with other agents so timings may be inflated.
- Because the verdict is REFUTED there is no cheapest design to report; the objective was irrelevant (no optimum).

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
My own python (work_c07/chk.py, plain floats), all 112 designs:
- H at the all-max corner (all HVL +10%, NK max; H is monotone increasing in all of them, so this is the true maximum) for lead 3 mm alone = 0.0103768, equal to the stand's upper spread 0.0103768. Match.
- Nominal H of lead 3 alone = 0.003957 < 0.005; so it passes at nominal and is the only design that satisfies R2 (mass <= 55 kg/m2, thickness <= 60) and R1 at nominal. Others satisfying mass/thickness fail at nominal (e.g. lead 2 + barite 10: 0.0162). Consistent with 111 failed_at_nominal. The nearest competitors by mass: lead 2.5 alone nominal 0.0149.
- Stand's violating point recomputed by hand from its listed HVLs: H = 0.00511051128, equal to the stand's value_there.
- No design satisfies R1 in the worst case together with R2 (checked all 112, none), so REFUTED is right. Dropping R2 (mass) only: cheapest passing is lead 2 mm + barite 30 mm (1876 EUR, worst H 0.003925, mass 103.7 kg/m2); dropping R3 changes nothing (no design passes R1+R2 anyway). This matches the stand's conflict set {R1, R2}: R3 is not in it.
- Not checked: the stand's lower spread end (0.0012592) and the largest_contributors list; the minimal_conflicts proof pieces for subsets other than I recomputed through my own check; ZTL's exactness itself.
- Caveat on the stand's answer: the witness is a poor witness (a mixed corner, not the worst corner) but valid; "closest" means the smallest overshoot found, not the worst-case overshoot, which is much larger (0.0104 vs 0.005).

## DOC GAPS
- BRICKS.md does not list the units Gy / Sv (and mGy, mSv) nor say to use J/kg; the list of known units has no radiation or time-per-week units. Suggested: add.
- The "20 toleranced quantities in one group of coupled laws: 2^n corners too many" limit is not in BRICKS.md (I do not know the real threshold: 20 refused, 16 accepted). The doc should state the limit and the exact-merge workaround (products of independent ranges).
- Nothing says that a REFUTED verdict triggers minimal_conflicts re-judging that multiplies the run time (here ~8 passes of ~2 min each); a REFUTED brick with a heavy checker can take far longer than the first pass suggests.
- Unary minus: examples use `0 - x`; the doc does not say whether `-x` is allowed (I followed the examples).
- The timeout behaviour: `timeout 900 ~/bin/heavy ...` ran 1041 s without being killed; the doc/wrapper is not mine to judge, but it is a finding.
