# c08 report: Electric delivery van, battery/tyres/aero/heater/optional solar (problem 8)

Verdict: EARNED, optimal. Design: pack B77 (77 kWh, 530 kg), ultra tyre (Crr 0.0072), full aero kit (0.86), heat pump (1.1 kW), no solar. Price 14.85 kEUR. The stand says `optimal: true` ("every cheaper design was refuted"). Only 2 of 216 designs pass: this one and the same plus solar (15.95 kEUR).

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- All catalogs as stated: 6 packs, 3 tyres, 3 aero options, 2 heaters, roof solar as an `optional` slot (catalog item kWp 0.9, 18 kg, 1.1 kEUR), 216 combinations.
- The four uncertain quantities are one shared given each (`CDA0` 1.05..1.15, `PAY` 500..850, `KR` 0.90..1.10, `FT` 0.82..0.92), used by all three routes in one brick. No modes were needed, so "common to all routes" is literal.
- Per route: `F = Crr*KR*m*g + 0.5*rho*CdA0*k*v^2`; kinetic loss `stops*0.5*m*v^2*(1-0.55)`; traction `(F+K)/0.88`; aux `(0.4 kW + heater)/v_avg` cold, `0.4 kW/v_avg` warm; range `U/E_cold`. Units km/h, 1/km, kWh, kW, Wh/km are given as stated and the stand converts them. I typed no conversion factors.
- `U = E_pack * exp(-0.028*8) * FT * 0.92 + 0.35 h * kWp`, with `exp` evaluated by the stand. The 0.35 kWh/kWp credit is a given in unit `h`, because kWh/kW = h.
- Consumption: `sum share*(0.5*Ec + 0.5*Ew)`, solar not counted.
- R1 (three route ranges >= daily distance) and R3 (`Mg == 2050 + pack + solar + 850 <= 3500`) are real derived quantities with requirements, so the stand judges them. R3 is a law about the 850 kg given `PAYMAX`, separate from the toleranced `PAY`. The objective is the sum of the five prices.
- Mass `m` is a `define` (`CURB + PACK.mass + PAY + SOL.mass`) containing the toleranced `PAY`.
