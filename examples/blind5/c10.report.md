# c10 report: aquaculture pond, aerators + seasonal schedule + LOX (problem 10)

Verdict: **EARNED**, optimal only inside a hand-narrowed space (see APPROXIMATED). Design: PW2 paddlewheel, n = 10,
running counts winter/spring/summer/autumn = 1 / 3 / 10 / 3, LOX unit installed. Cost 87.9496 kEUR
(36 + 6.5 + 10 * 28406 kWh * 0.16 EUR/kWh). Energy 28406 kWh, load 20 kW. Final run 31 s.

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Four seasons as four `modes`; each mode overrides the ranges of W and T (independent per season, one T per season
  used in every formula: Cs, kd, r, aerator transfer share the same T).
- Cs(T) as `table(...)` over the 7 knots (linear interpolation; T 8..11 spans the knot at 10, handled as a branch).
- r, kd, the aerator transfer coefficient with their `1.08^(T-20)`, `1.065^(T-20)`, `1.024^(T-20)` as `pow` with
  non-constant exponent; the 0.90 and 0.95 factors and 9.09 mg/L; units all declared (g/(kg*h), mg/L, kW, kg/kWh...),
  unit check passed ("checked: every law's dimensions agree").
- Steady state C_ss solved algebraically (see below) and R1 (>= 4.0 per season, worst case over the box, T worst-case
  not assumed at an end: certificates split the box; "certificate checked exactly" appeared in `how`).
- Power-cut closed form with `exp(-kd*t2)`, LOX term `ox = rate/V`, floor `max(., 0)`, evaluated at t = 2 h; R2 >= 3.0.
- Aerator type (4 listed items with P, SAE, price), n = 1..16 (integers), LOX yes/no (item "none" with zero fields +
  item "LOX": 6.5 kEUR, 6000 g/h), four running counts as four integer slots.
- R3 and R4 as real requirements (derived `Ey`, `Load`, bound by givens `Emax`, `Pmax`), judged by the stand:
  proved by a variant with Emax = 28000 (work_c10/emax28000.json): REFUTED, `minimal_conflicts` = {R3}, witness
  Ey = 28406, overshoot 406.
- Objective: 10-year cost = prices + 10 * annual energy * 0.16 as `computed`, with units (kEUR).

## APPROXIMATED
1. **Design space narrowed by hand (NOT safe to skip; only I checked the logic, the stand checked the facts it relies on).**
   The full space is 4 types * 2 LOX * 16 n * 17^4 running counts = 10.7 M combinations; the stand enumerates the
   whole product before `where` and did not print anything in 19 min (see TOOL OUTPUT). I therefore:
   - `where: N.k == max(MW.k, MS.k, MU.k, MA.k)` (n larger than the largest running count is dominated: costs more,
     R4 harder, nothing gained). Argument is mine, not checked by the stand.
   - Per (type, LOX) and season a window `m in [m_min, m_min+1]`, where m_min is the smallest count that satisfies
     R1 and R2 for that season. I got m_min from my own numpy model (grid over T, corners of W) and then **verified
     with the stand** in 4 single-season bricks (work_c10/sub_*.json): for all 8 (type, LOX) x 4 seasons the stand
     REFUTED m_min-1 and EARNED m_min (PROP/summer: m = 16 REFUTED both with and without LOX, so PROP has no
     feasible summer count within n <= 16; its window is {16}). So the part "m < m_min is infeasible" IS stand-checked
     (in separate runs). The part "m > m_min+1 is never better" rests on monotonicity (more aerators running = more
     energy and cost, and R1/R2 are only helped) - my argument only. Safe, but unproven by the stand.
   - Catalog ranges for slots narrowed: N 6..13, winter 1..2, spring 2..5, summer 6..13, autumn 2..6 (all contain every
     window). Same status as above.
   - Consequence: the stand's `optimal: true` ("every cheaper design was refuted") is true only within this space;
     `designs_tried: 1` because the cheapest surviving design was EARNED at once. The 20384 excluded combinations are
     excluded by me, not judged.
2. **C_ss solved by hand**: the problem says "solve a linear equation for C". The law form needs `X == expression`,
   so I wrote C_ss = ((kd + 0.95 a) Cs - r)/(kd + a) with a = m P SAE 0.90 theta^(T-20)/(9.09 V) (derived by me; the
   stand does not see the balance equation itself). I checked it by substitution in python (model.py) and it agrees
   with the stand's refutation magnitudes. Nobody checks the algebra but me (and my check script).
3. **LOX duration "up to 2 h"** is not modelled; I use the 2 h evaluation only (same as the problem's R2 evaluation).
4. Cs, kd, r, a, C_ss are `define`s (functions of T, W substituted in place) and not derived quantities, to avoid the
   interval-dependency blow-up. Safe; the stand bounds the resulting definitions (interval reading + certificates).
5. The `max(., 0)` floor is kept as stated (not approximated).
6. 10 years is a plain number 10 (no discounting, as stated). 24000 m^3, 0.16 EUR/kWh given with units.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- A decision of the form "m_s <= n" and "n >= max m" as a *dominance-free* constraint is fine in `where`, but there is
  no way to say "choose four counts without the 17^4 product" (a per-season independent choice / decomposition).
  The format enumerates the product of slot catalogs before `where`; there is no lazy/pruned search.
- `optional` slot together with `modes` (see below) - had to replace the optional LOX by a two-item catalog with a
  zero item. Not a loss of meaning.
- The annual-energy sum as a *total over modes* with `totals` was avoidable only because energy has no uncertainty;
  I computed it from catalog values in one law (`Ey`), so it is repeated in every mode (harmless duplicate).
- Nothing else of the physics was unsayable.

  (same message for slot name LX). Same brick without `modes` ran. So `optional: true` combined with `modes` is a bug
  (probably the brick is prepared once per mode and the second time the catalog already exists).

  `timeout 900 ~/bin/heavy ...` did **not** kill the python child after 900 s; I killed it by PID (`kill -9`) at 19:35.

  ~30 s for an EARNED design (4 modes with certificates: summer C2 needed 862 pieces).

- Final brick (c10.json, 96 designs after `where`, 20384 excluded): **EARNED**, 31 s, `designs_tried 1`, nothing OPEN.
  PW2/LOX n=10, m = (1,3,10,3). `how`: Css winter/spring "interval reading of the definition (sound, may be wide)",
  everything else "certificate checked exactly" (pieces: 2, 8, 1, 862, 2, 20), re-checked by the checker.
  objective value 109937/1250 = 87.9496 kEUR, optimal: true (within the narrowed space).
- Variant Emax = 28000: REFUTED, 96 designs, 171 s, conflict set {R3}.
- Older variant `work_c10/c10_v1_where_energy.json` (R3, R4 as `where`): also EARNED, 32 s, same design.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
- Independent numpy model (work_c10/model.py, T grid 61-201 points, W corners): minimal running counts per type/LOX/season
  identical to the stand's sub-brick results in all 32 cases. Cost 10*3.6 + 6.5 + 10*28406*0.16/1000 = 87.9496 (matches).
  Energy: 2*(90*12*1 + 92*10*3 + 92*8*10 + 91*11*3) = 2*14203 = 28406 (matches `Ey`).
- Why the others lose (hand): PW2 without LOX needs summer m = 12 -> 31350 kWh > 31000 (R3 fails by 350 - the
  tight point of the problem); PW37 and DIFF need >= 34550 kWh even with LOX; PROP cannot hold summer at all (m = 16
  gives Css 3.83, C2 2.91). So the unique feasible (type, LOX) is PW2 + LOX. I did not check this by stand over the full
  space - only by the windows and the sub-bricks.
- The stand's ranges enclose mine: e.g. winter Css [5.62, 8.81] vs my worst-case grid [6.41, 7.95]; summer C2 lower
  bound 3 (the requirement) vs my 3.124; margins are not tight (summer Css 4.06 vs 4.0 is the closest).
- Not checked: the problem's own ambiguity whether n must exceed the running counts at each season (I assumed n >= m_s,
  and n = max m); the stand's certificate pieces (trusted, they are rechecked by its checker).

## DOC GAPS
- BRICKS.md does not say that all slot catalogs are enumerated as a full product BEFORE `where`, so a `where` cannot
  rescue a large catalog (here 10.7 M combinations -> silent for 19 min with no progress line). It says "the count is the
  product" but suggests `where` "makes a large catalog small".
- `optional: true` + `modes` crashes with the 'OX_none is taken' BRICK ERROR; not documented.
- `timeout 900 ~/bin/heavy` leaves the child running past 900 s (tooling, not BRICKS.md, but the guide's run line
  should use `timeout -s KILL` or heavy's own limit).
- `designs_tried` / `optimal: true` do not say that `where`-excluded designs were never judged (the note "every cheaper
  design was refuted" is vacuous when `designs_tried` is 1).
- It is not stated whether `table` y-values given as bare numbers carry units; I avoided it with `Cu*table(...)`.
- A per-mode `define` replacing a top-level define that other defines already use (`mrun` inside `a` inside `Cssd`)
  works (late binding), but the guide only mentions it for an expression, not for the nested case.
