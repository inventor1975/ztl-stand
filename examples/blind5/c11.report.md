## APPROXIMATED
1. **S_q enumerated as 5 point scenarios per quarter (modes), not as a range. NOT SAFE by itself, checked only by me.** 5^4 = 625 modes, each with S1..S4 fixed at {1.02, 1.06, 1.10, 1.14, 1.22}; v stays a range. Reason: the continuous LP takes each coefficient's worst case separately; profit's S-dependent coefficients (v/S, 1/F-1/S, 1/min(S,K)-1/S) are then decoupled, and with S as a range the LP found no mix at all (first run: OPEN, 4/4 "no mix, Farkas not below zero"). Point scenarios make the LP exact. The claim "worst case over the whole interval is at one of these points" is MY argument: for fixed x profit is piecewise monotone in S with breakpoint only at K, so min is at lo, K or hi; K is one of the 3 strikes, hence the 5 points. The stand does not check this. The kink really matters: the binding scenario in the output is S3 = 1.10 (= K), with A = 0.3000001.
2. **Independent S_q for R2 handled by the full product (625 modes)** instead of "sum of per-quarter minima"; exact under (1) and correct but heavy.
3. **No-options case**: a catalog item with field `on = 0`, pi = 0; the option fraction used everywhere is `on*o` (the slot O_q still exists as a free variable, irrelevant). Derived G_q = h_q + on*o_q carries R4/R3. Stand checks it; it is just a modelling device.
4. R3 rewritten by hand as sum(h+o) in [1.6, 3.2]. Exact, hand algebra.
5. Continuum verification (extra, not in c11.json; files in `work_c11/`): the stand cannot decide the true box (S as ranges, h,o fixed) for A: OPEN ("extreme may lie inside the box and no certificate decided it"). R1 per quarter and R3-R6 are EARNED in the true box. For R2 I used a decomposition BY HAND (A's minimum = sum of quarter minima, because every P_q is increasing in v, so v = 0.85 is the worst for all quarters at once; then four single-quarter bricks `wA1..wA4` each EARNED with bound = quarter min - 0.002; the bounds sum to 0.30001). That design is a slightly costlier one (cost 54.15 kEUR, o4 = 0.6216, R2 slack 0.008), because the stand's certificate would not close with a margin of 1e-8 (open for margins 1e-7 .. about 1e-4, closed at 0.12 vs min 0.1299, and with 0.002 margin). So for the exact LP optimum, the continuum is checked only by my calculation, not the stand.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- A range S_q inside the continuous LP with a non-decoupled worst case (the robust LP treats every coefficient's worst case independently; here one S drives 3 coefficients). Only workaround: scenario modes (approximation 1).
- "Strike is the same in all modes and the scenario S equals K": a mode's `value` cannot be a catalog field, so I put all three possible K values in the grid. A grid depending on the chosen item is not available.
- "Independent per quarter" together with "shared v" within a continuous brick without 625 modes. (Modes-as-quarters with `totals` is conceivable; I did not try it because the amounts H_q differ per quarter and mode laws would be needed. Not tested, so no claim.)
- A proof for the interior kink inside the LP: the stand decides interior extremes only for a fixed design, not for continuous amounts.
