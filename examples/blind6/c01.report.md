## APPROXIMATED
1. kt (termination efficiency) is one quantity used in all classes. The text does not say "shared". Safe: damage and SFC are monotone in kt, so the worst case is kt = 0.88 in every class at once; independent draws per class could not be worse. Checked by: only me (reasoning); the stand sees one value.
2. Payload within a class: one value m_i in the range per class (not a draw per lift). Safe because damage is monotone increasing in m and the worst case is every lift of the class at the top. Only me.
3. "each lift anywhere in the range" not modelled as a distribution; the sup is what is proved.
4. `/1000` in F dropped: F is declared in kN and the law gives N, the unit scaling does the conversion (BRICKS 5 forbids a bare factor). Exact, checked by the stand's unit check.
5. Life >= 10 years written as Dm <= 0.10 per year (the problem's own "i.e."). Exact.
6. Optimality is "within a gap": the stand returns D = 0.2114812 m (a hair inside the Dm bound) and a proved lower bound 1484.38; `optimal: false` at a gap of 0.0085 EUR. Not my change, but the answer is a mix inside 0.0085 EUR of the optimum, not the exact optimum. Checked by ZTL (zmin).
7. R3 and R4 and the catalog window are all requirements/ranges the stand judges; nothing was moved to `where`. No hand algebra, no bounds computed outside.
