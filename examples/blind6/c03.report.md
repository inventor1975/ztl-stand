# c03 report: cordless saw, cell pack + BMS + optional heater at -10 degC (problem 3)

Files: `c03.json` (the design question, flat brick, ambient -10 degC). Scratch in `work_c03/`: `c03_find.json`
(the two limit questions, `find` list), `gen.py` (writes both bricks), `system.json` + `pack.json` (components
version), `brute.py` (my own corner enumeration), `run_*.out` (raw outputs).

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- All five requirements R1..R5 as stated (R3 bound = the BMS field `BMS.Irat`; R4 as margin `Imarg = Imax(Tc) - I/Np >= 0`).
- Cell type (5 listed items), Np = 1..4 (integer catalog), BMS (4 items), heater as an OPTIONAL slot with
  dTh in [9, 13] K, 3.5 Wh, 4.5 EUR. 160 designs; objective `5*Np*cell price + BMS price + heater price`.
- Uncertain data as ranges: P 540..640 W, Voc 3.46..3.52 V, a 1.00..1.10, B 2000..2400 K, capacity factor 0.97..1.03,
  BMS factor 1.0..1.2; wiring 4 mohm fixed. The Arrhenius law with `exp`, the cold capacity factor with `max(0.5, ...)`,
  the cell current limit, usable energy minus the heater draw, runtime = E/P (no unit factor typed: E in Wh, P in W,
  Runtime in min).
- The constant-power load in closed form: `Vt = (V0 + sqrt(V0^2 - 4 Rtot P))/2`, `I = P/Vt` (same I as the problem's root),
  with `valid: V0*V0 > 4*Rtot*P` stating the stall condition.
- Shared quantities (B, a, Voc, capacity factor, BMS factor, dTh) are ONE quantity each, used in every law.
- Limit question 1 (lowest ambient with ANY design) and 2 (for the chosen design) by `find` ("smallest" on Ta).

## APPROXIMATED
1. **`sqrt(max(0, V0*V0 - 4*Rtot*P))` instead of plain `sqrt(...)`.** Without it the stand crashes on any design that
   stalls at the nominal point (STAND ERROR, see TOOL OUTPUT). With the guard a stalled design gets Vt = V0/2 ~ 8.7 V,
   which violates R1 (14.2 V), so the verdict is the same as "no solution = failure"; the `valid` requirement is also kept.
   Safe (conservative: it can only turn a stall into a failure). Checked by the stand (R1 fails with overshoot 219/40 = 14.2 - 8.725 on every
   stalled design, as it should); my brute force agrees on all 160 designs.
2. **Ambient fixed at -10 degC in the main brick** (the problem says the cold end is worst for every requirement). Not
   assumed blindly: I ran the same catalog with Ta as the range 263.15..313.15 K (`work_c03/v_range.json`): the stand
   gives the same two EARNED designs and the same lower guaranteed ends (Vt >= 14.757, Runtime >= 10.3836, ...). The
   stand checked it; the main brick relies on it only for speed.
3. **Temperatures in K, not degC.** The exponential needs an absolute temperature, so Ta = 263.15 K, Tref = 298.15 K,
   slopes 0.007 and 0.012 as `1/K`, and (25 - Tc) written as (Tref - Tc). Exact, nothing lost.
4. **Capacity "0.97 to 1.03 of nominal" read as ONE factor for the whole pack.** If each cell could differ the worst case
   (all at 0.97) is the same, so safe for the minimum-runtime requirement. Only I checked this reading.
5. **Limits by `find` on the range [a, -10 degC]**: the stand asks "some design works for EVERY ambient in [a, -10]", the
   problem asks "a design works AT a". Equal because every requirement is monotone in temperature (the range run in item 2
   and my brute force show it for this catalog) -- I verified this numerically, the stand does not state it.
6. **Limit for the chosen design: a pin hack.** `find` cannot fix a design, so the second find item carries the five
   requirements plus four pins as requirements on catalog fields (`CELL.Imax25 within [20,20]`, `NP.N within [4,4]`,
   `BMS.Irat within [60,60]`, `HT.Eh within [3.5,3.5]`) that REFUTE every other combination. The design printed in `at_proved` is
   H25 / 4 / M60 / film, so the pin worked; it relies on those field values being unique to that design (I checked by hand).
7. Find precision 0.01 K, so the limits are grid values: 257.03 K is "proved", 257.02 K "beyond".
8. 152 of the 160 designs are REFUTED at the nominal point (a concrete point recomputed exactly) without a certificate;
   legitimate, but it means only 7-8 designs were really judged over the box.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- **"No solution = failure" as a plain domain.** `valid` exists, but the stand evaluates the formula at the nominal
  point first and crashes on a negative radicand; the `max(0, .)` guard is a workaround, not the problem's statement.
- **Design question and limit question in one run.** A brick with `find` answers only the find (the design appears as `at_proved`);
  c03.json therefore has no `find` and the limits live in `work_c03/c03_find.json`. Two files for one problem.
- **"Fix this design, find the limit"** has no direct syntax (item 6 above).
- **The chain as components, with the load in it.** The constant-power controller couples the pack and the BMS through the current (a
  feedback); components are one-way, so the load had to stay in the system's laws and the BMS stayed a system slot (see TOOL OUTPUT, components).
- An optional part inside a component, and `valid` on a law of a system whose inputs are component outputs (both crash, below).

## TOOL OUTPUT
- First run of the brick as first written (plain `sqrt`), also with `valid` removed, and with one stalling design alone:
  `STAND ERROR: ValueError: unknown quantity or malformed arithmetic: 'Vt' (at znumjudge.py:245)` after "1/160 designs, 0 s".
  A message about 'Vt' for what is a negative square root: misleading. Fixed by the `max(0, .)` guard (approximation 1).
- **c03.json (flat, final), work mode:** EARNED, 70.00 EUR, `optimal: true` ("every cheaper design was refuted"): cell H25
  (2.5 Ah, 17 mohm, 20 A), Np = 4 (20 cells), BMS M60, heater fitted. 159 designs tried, 152 failed at nominal, 7 judged, 0 undecided.
  Guaranteed ranges (all `ZTL, interval reading`): Vt [14.757, 16.135] V (R1 >= 14.2), Runtime [10.384, 13.553] min
  (>= 9), I [33.47, 43.37] A (<= 60), Imarg [2.918, 6.353] A (>= 0), Vcell [3.0225, 3.2773] V (>= 2.9). Wall time 0.45 s.
- `--dev` (independent checker): same verdict, same design, 0.43 s, no STAND FAULT.
- `"report": "all"`: exactly two EARNED designs: H25/4/M60/film (70.0) and H25/4/M80/film (73.5). 0.45 s.
- Full ambient range -10..+40 degC: same two EARNED, same optimum, 0.65 s.
- **Limits (`work_c03/c03_find.json`, 9.6 s, 13 and 14 tries):**
  (1) any design: smallest Ta proved 257.03 K = **-16.12 degC** (design H25/4/M80/film; `beyond` 257.02 K REFUTED with a concrete failing point);
  (2) chosen design H25/4/M60/film: **257.61 K = -15.54 degC** (`beyond` 257.60 K REFUTED).
- **Components version** (`work_c03/system.json` + `pack.json`): the PACK component holds cell, Np and heater as its OWN slots
  (cost = 5*Np*price + heater), contract input Ta with `bands: "arriving"`, outputs Rpk, V0, Rc, E, Imaxc; the BMS is a system slot;
  the load/Vt/I/Vcell/R1..R5 are system laws. Result: EARNED, 70 EUR, H25 / Np 4 / film + M60, guaranteed ranges IDENTICAL to the flat brick,
  9.3 s (20x slower). `optimal: false`, and the cheaper pack/BMS combinations are listed OPEN ("a system cannot refute")
  although the flat run refutes them. Problems met on the way: (a) `"optional": true` on a slot inside a component:
  `STAND ERROR: KeyError: 'HT_none' (at components.py:185)` -- worked around by an explicit "none" heater item; (b) `valid` on a system law that uses
  component outputs: `BRICK ERROR: quantities.valid_1.unit: must be a string` -- dropped (the max guard remains). The `find` over the system
  (`system_find.json`) gives the same 257.03 K but 2 m 06 s, and its `beyond` is OPEN, not REFUTED.
- No run came near the 3 GB / 900 s limits.

## DOES THE ANSWER LOOK RIGHT
My own script `work_c03/brute.py` (python floats, every corner of the 7 uncertain quantities, 2^7 for the heater case; the requirement values
are monotone in each datum, so corners give the worst case):
- Passing designs: exactly H25/4/M60/film (70.0 EUR) and H25/4/M80/film (73.5 EUR); the 158 others fail -- same as the stand's `report: all`.
- Worst values for the chosen design: Vt 14.757, Runtime 10.384, I 43.369, Imarg 2.918, Vcell 3.023: equal to the stand's ends to 3 decimals
  (its upper ends I did not compare). Tightest requirement: R4 margin 2.9 A and R1 margin 0.56 V; R3 has 17 A spare.
- Limits by bisection on Ta: any design 257.029 K (-16.121 degC, H25/4/M80/film; R1 binds), chosen design 257.609 K (-15.541 degC, R1 binds
  at Vt = 14.2000). The stand's grid values 257.03 / 257.61 sit on the safe side of those. Match.
- Hand sanity of the worst corner of the chosen design (a 1.10, B 2400 K, dTh 9 K, P 640 W, Voc 3.46 V, BMS x1.2): Tc = 272.15 K,
  Rcell = 1.1*17*exp(2400*(1/272.15 - 1/298.15)) = 40.3 mohm, Rtot = 5*40.3/4 + 3.5*1.2 + 4 = 58.6 mohm, V0 = 17.3 V,
  I = 43.4 A, Vt = 14.76 V: matches the stand's Vt >= 14.757 and I <= 43.369.
- NOT checked: my brute force and the stand use the same reading of the statement, so a misreading (e.g. kT floor, shared capacity factor) would be
  in both; the stand's `max`/`exp` bracket I did not check beyond the match above; no check of designs the stand refuted at nominal other than through the brute force.

## DOC GAPS
- BRICKS.md section 4 says `valid` makes a formula's domain a requirement, but not that the formula is first EVALUATED at the nominal point,
  where a negative square root is a STAND ERROR with an unrelated message ('Vt'). A guarded form (`max(0, .)`) should be recommended there.
- Not stated that `max(0, x)` takes its 0 in x's unit (it works with x in V^2), and that a define may hold `max`.
- Section 7a: a brick with `find` returns ONLY the find, no design verdict; the way to ask "limit for a given design" (pinning by `within [v, v]` on
  catalog fields) is not documented and I found it by trying.
- Section 7d: optional slot in a component and `valid` in a system law are not listed as unsupported; both crash. The system `optimal: false`
  consequence with ~160 candidates is documented, but the cost (20x slower than the flat brick here) is not.
- Not said that an optional slot's heater range field (`dTh` as `range`) in the "none" item becomes exactly 0 (it does).
