- Exponent 0.35 written as 7/20 (exact). Safe.
- "or none" as an explicit catalog item, not `optional` (an absent part would get k = 0, which is wrong for a multiplier). Safe; my modelling.
- R2 (tau <= Tov) is redundant in the brick: the tau slot already ends at 2190 = Tov. Stated as a requirement anyway; it cannot bite.
- Objective is the stand's NOMINAL objective; the annual cost has no toleranced term, so no loss.
- The admissible tau interval is NOT in c12.json: `find` searches a GIVEN, not an amount of a continuous slot. I made a second brick (generated copy of the same laws, tau as a given "tau", the two parts pinned to TL + monitoring, `find` smallest and largest). Consistency of the two bricks is by my script, not checked by the stand. Safe if the script is right (I diffed the laws by eye).
- `find` over a tau RANGE is sound but loose here (see TOOL OUTPUT): proved [866, 1348.6] h only, and the 900 s limit stopped the run. To report the limits I ran four point checks (tau = 788, 788.1, 1376.7, 1376.8, tau as a plain given): REFUTED, EARNED, EARNED, REFUTED. That brackets the limits to 0.1 h. The claim "everything between 788.1 and 1376.7 is admissible" rests on my own argument (worst-case U(tau) = A tau + B + D/tau is convex, worst corner = lam0 high, T high, H high, k = 0.92, c low, td high), not on a stand proof; the stand proved ranges [866, 1300] and [1300, 1348.6] only.

## DOC GAPS

- 7a "a slow brick times ~15 tries": here a 1-design brick took 30 to 110 s per try on a wide range (the interval reading of an unavailability formula with 10 toleranced data), so two finds do not fit in 900 s. Nothing says that a find range is much slower and looser than a point run; the stop at the time limit returning a bracket is not documented either.
- Section on temperatures: "C is a difference" is said, but the usual need (degC data entering `exp(B(1/Tref - 1/T))`) is not shown; one has to know to write K and add 273.15 to range ends.
- `optimal: false` at a 9e-5 gap is explained in the output; in the continuous section a small gap is described only for equality "zero gap". OK but the user will wonder.
- No statement how a non-affine OBJECTIVE (a ratio 8760/tau) is handled in the continuous section: "Amounts NOT affine" mentions requirements and an objective in passing; it worked without trouble.
