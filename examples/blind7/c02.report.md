# c02 report: Yellow and red-clearance timing (problem 02)

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Givens: t = 1 s, a = 10, L = 20, posted limit 55 (all exact); V in [47, 52], g in [-0.04, -0.02], W in [112, 120] as independent ranges.
- Y formula `Yf = t + 1.47*V/(2a + 64.4 g)` and R formula `Rf = (W+L)/(1.47*VR) - 1` with `VR = min(V, 55)` as `define`s. The "R never below 1.0 s" floor is `Rreq = max(Rf, 1 s)`.
- Y and R are decisions made once: slots with integer catalogs, Y = N/10 with N in 30..60 (3.0-6.0 s) and R = N/10 with N in 10..60 (1.0-6.0 s). This gives "multiple of 0.1 s" and the min/max rules.
- "At least the formula value, rounded up to 0.1 s" is the requirement `Y - Yf >= 0` and `R - Rreq >= 0` for every V, g, W. Because the setting is a multiple of 0.1, this is equivalent to the rounded-up rule.
- Total intergreen `Y + R <= 6.4` as a requirement.
- Objective: minimize N_Y + N_R (smallest admissible settings).
- Two bricks: `c02a.json` has no total requirement and answers "smallest admissible settings". `c02.json` adds the 6.4 s budget and answers "does any (Y,R) meet everything, and which rules conflict".

## APPROXIMATED
- Units: V, g, W, a, L are dimensionless numbers (mph and ft are not stand units, and the formula mixes them). Unit carriers `su`, `one`, `dt` (1 s, 1 s, 0.1 s) make the results seconds. The stand's dimension check passed. Nobody checked the mph/ft conversion constants; they are the problem's own.
- "Minimize Y+R" is used as the meaning of "smallest settings". Here Y and R are independent (the minimum of each is attained together), so it does not matter. I checked that in my script.
- Slider-free; nothing else approximated.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- Rounding up as a function (ceil to 0.1) of the uncertain value: not expressible as a law because ceil of an uncertain quantity is not available. I used the equivalent "setting >= formula value" with settings on the 0.1 grid. No loss.
- The conflict size in seconds is not a stand output; it gives the minimal conflict set only. I computed the numbers myself (below).
