# c12 report: Choosing projects under a capital budget (problem 12)

## EXPRESSED FAITHFULLY
- Seven projects A..G, each as its own optional slot (PA..PG) with a one-item catalog: investment exact, NPV = listed value with tolerance 0.05 (95..105 %). Absent project = auto-generated `none` item (zeros).
- Budget 200 MUSD as a given; the budget constraint is a `where` on the exact investments (sum inv <= Budget).
- Law: NPV == sum of the seven project NPVs. Requirement: NPV >= 74.0 (MUSD), worst case.
- Objective: minimize -(sum of nominal NPVs), i.e. maximise total NPV. `report: all`.
- NPVs vary independently (the stand's default for separate givens).

## APPROXIMATED
- The objective is NOMINAL (the format has no "maximise the worst case" for a bound requirement). I used the nominal sum. This is safe here because every NPV is scaled by the same 0.95..1.05 relative range, so the worst case is exactly 0.95 x nominal and the order of designs is the same. Checked by me (check.py), not by the stand. For non-uniform tolerances it would not be safe.
- The "maximum worst-case NPV" itself is not reported as an objective value (it reports nominal 78); the worst-case value comes from `guaranteed` lower end.

## COULD NOT EXPRESS
- "Maximise the worst case" as a first-class objective with a requirement of the same quantity: `case: worst` minimises an upper bound, so it cannot maximise a lower bound. Worked around via the nominal objective above.
- Margin in percent of the requirement: not output by the stand; computed by hand.
